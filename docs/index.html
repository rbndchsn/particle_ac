Particle Acceleration: The Instrument And Its Blind Spot

Six places where the most precisely confirmed science on Earth rests on something nobody has ever checked. Assembled from the field's own papers, audits and court records.

Robin2026-11-251043 tier-labelled claims

Modern particle physics presents itself as the most rigorously verified body of knowledge humans have produced, and at its core it is. That is not the finding. The finding is what sits around that core — a structure in which the field's most expensive and least constrained claims are protected not by evidence but by the fact that nobody has looked. In six separate places, the quantity that would settle a load-bearing question has never been computed, and in every case the absence is documented in the field's own literature.

What that structure permits becomes visible once it is assembled in one place. A criterion carrying a published failure condition, which was met, conceded in the journals, and then stripped of its vocabulary in the documents requesting the next machine. An instrument that destroys thirteen thousand times more data than it keeps, in hardware, irreversibly, under a menu built from the theories it exists to test. Two experiments presented as independent confirmation of each other, whose simulated physics descends from a single manual fit. A literature that has released full statistical likelihoods for two analyses, and that contains machine-learning results its own authors state cannot be reproduced outside the collaboration that made them. An institution that names, in its own technical papers, the precise conditions under which a sole supplier could overcharge it — while publishing no audit output, no senior remuneration, no register of interests, and standing immune from the jurisdiction in which a challenge could be heard.

None of this requires that anyone lied. It requires only that incentives aligned and that nobody was positioned to check — a more durable problem than fraud, and a harder one to repair. This is a brief for that case, argued from one side and saying so, assembled almost entirely out of CERN technical papers, Inspector General audits, GAO reports, collaboration reinterpretation inventories and court records. Roughly eighty claims tested here failed, including both hypotheses the project began with. They are kept on the record, because an argument built only from what survived would be worth nothing.

Six questions the field cannot answer#

Not disputed. Unasked. In each case the quantity that would settle it has never been computed, and the absence is documented.

01

How much physics does the trigger throw away?#

The LHC discards 13,000× of its collisions in hardware, in microseconds, unrecoverably — under a menu built, in the field's own words, from "theoretical models and detailed Monte Carlo simulations." Trigger error is counted in one direction: keeping too much costs budgeted bandwidth everyone can see; discarding the wrong thing costs events that no longer exist.

No coverage integral over signature space has ever been published.

02

Why doesn't the lineshape match?#

The electron magnetic moment is the showpiece — 0.13 parts per trillion. Its largest systematic is a cyclotron lineshape that does not match prediction, in the measuring group's own words. The same group advises against averaging its own 2008 and 2023 results.

Nobody outside that group has investigated it.

03

What is the stopping power pinned to?#

Electronic stopping power is the dominant uncertainty in ion-beam analysis — 0.8% inside a 1% budget — and it is calibrated against the very standard it is then used to measure.

No published multi-energy consistency check exists.

04

How independent were the two experiments in 2012?#

Both LHC experiments' hadronisation descends from one manual fit whose authors describe making "(subjective) decisions" and set a parameter to "effectively mimic" a missing calculation. Correlation scans exist for the top mass and the tt̄ cross section.

No scan has ever been published for the discovery significance itself — the one quantity where independence was load-bearing.

05

What does the superconductor actually cost?#

CERN's own papers name the "risk and high cost of sole supplier" for Nb₃Sn, a 40% weight-price premium, degrading yield at the single qualified vendor, and $13/kA·m against $7.50 in 2005. One supplier met the HL-LHC specification. Eight met ITER's.

That pricing has never been benchmarked or audited.

06

Who checked the showpiece calculation?#

The five-loop QED coefficient was wrong from 2019 to 2024 — by about five times its own quoted uncertainty — because 98 of 6,354 diagrams were under-sampled. It was caught only because one person outside the originating group redid it.

There are still only two independent computations. Agreement between two catches an error in one, not an error common to both.

The argument#

Particle physics published, in advance and in print, the condition under which its central organising argument would fail. The condition was met. The argument was then removed from the documents that ask for the next machine — without being retracted in them.

Around that absence sits a system that cannot audit what it discards, and whose accountability is weakest exactly where its claims are strongest.

The criterion that failed its own test#

In 2008 a senior theorist derived an explicit bound — new physics below roughly 1 TeV — and wrote that if the LHC found none, the naturalness criterion would fail. It found none. By 2017 the same author conceded it: a doctor unable to find the right diagnosis cannot simply declare the patient healed.

The 2023 US strategy report contains zero instances of "naturalness," "hierarchy problem," "fine-tuning" or "unnatural" across all 379,741 characters — while still paraphrasing the argument those words name. The 2025 European briefing book restores the term.

Meanwhile the quantitative threshold defining "natural" was raised by stipulation three times across three decades, while the exclusion limits it was meant to predict climbed by nearly an order of magnitude.

What the instrument destroys#

Rate is allocated by committee, annually, in roughly equal thirds between searches, measurements and flavour physics. CMS states plainly that it lacked a particular trigger, which "prevented the measurements of R_K and R_K*" — prevented, not delayed.

What the literature cannot check#

The field's own reinterpretation inventory records full statistical likelihoods released for two analyses. Efficiency maps in "very few cases." Data for refits "often not provided." Machine-learning analyses that "cannot be reproduced at all outside the experimental collaboration." By 2025, the reproducibility of some earlier searches had been lost inside those collaborations.

Authorship is earned by a service threshold — eighty eight-hour days and a year — after which a name appears on every paper. The committee that grants it explicitly disclaims responsibility for content.

The money, and what is not knowable about it#

The official cost-benefit analysis assigns 1.7% of the LHC's €16.4bn benefit to scientific knowledge, and 66% to spillover and human capital. Both it and the UK national evaluation monetise the physics by counting papers.

An Inspector General report questioned $162,018,139.47 of $413.9m claimed by Fermilab's operator for a single fiscal year, $159.47m of it unsupported indirect costs — and found that DOE "did not review or approve" the contractor's indirect cost rates. On the SSC, on the same date in 1993, DOE's own estimate showed a $124m overrun against an independent Departmental assessment of ~$1.6bn. A factor of thirteen.

Three named LHC suppliers carry criminal or competition findings in other markets: Alstom pleaded guilty to FCPA books-and-records falsification at $772.29m; Furukawa Electric to Sherman Act bid rigging and price fixing at $200m, with three executives jailed; Air Liquide and Linde, the world's only two qualified helium turbo-expander makers, were both named in a Brazilian market-allocation case.

And the finding that outlasts all of them.

CERN's own technical papers document the conditions under which overcharging would be possible and hard to detect — sole supplier, degrading yield, a 40% premium, a price that has nearly doubled since 2005. CERN publishes its non-competitive-award threshold but not the count or value. It publishes no internal audit output, no senior remuneration, no register of personnel interests, no post-employment restriction. It is immune from national jurisdiction, so a bid protest cannot exist.

Both halves are sourced to the institution itself. This is not an allegation that money was taken. It is a demonstration that the question cannot be answered from outside — which is a harder thing to dismiss, and a harder thing to fix.

Who has actually challenged this field#

Sixty documented confrontations since 1961. Philip Anderson told the Senate in 1989 that high-energy physics received roughly ten times more per capita than other fields and that officials were "deliberately misleading Congress." The Superconducting Super Collider died 282–143. Seven legal proceedings across four jurisdictions produced zero merits rulings — every court declined to reach the physics, which is not the same as finding it safe.

What this brief does not claim#

The machines work. The Standard Model is the most precisely confirmed theory in the history of physics, and nothing here disputes a physical result. Fabrication was examined and rejected. Particle physics is not a cost-overrun outlier — JWST and ITER are far worse. No prosecution, debarment or bid protest arising from a CERN contract exists anywhere in the record.

Those findings are on the record with the rest, at full length, because the case above is only worth something if they are.
