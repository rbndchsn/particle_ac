The answer, stated before the evidence#

Yes. At least 60 distinct, documented, dated confrontations with the legitimacy of particle accelerator programmes can be identified from primary and scholarly sources, spanning 1961 to 2026. They include five Nobel laureates in physics, two national science advisory bodies, seven separate legal proceedings in four jurisdictions, a 27-organisation environmental coalition, and a sitting President-elect of the American Physical Society.

But the headline number is not the finding. The finding is the distribution.

Category What is challenged Count
(a) Validity the physics results are wrong or do not show what is claimed 3
(b) Priority / cost the physics is fine; the money is not justified 21
(c) Safety the machines are physically dangerous 12
(d) Epistemological / sociological the status of the knowledge produced 15
(e) Specific result one claimed finding is wrong 9

Three out of sixty. And of those three, zero are practising particle physicists, zero appeared in a peer-reviewed venue, and zero engage the measurement that would falsify them A. Every one of the 36 scientists in categories (b) and (d) - including Philip Anderson, Nicolaas Bloembergen, J. Robert Schrieffer, Chen-Ning Yang, Burton Richter, Sheldon Glashow, Lee Smolin, Peter Woit and Sabine Hossenfelder - accepts, in writing, that the machines work and that the Standard Model is confirmed A. The organised environmental opposition to the Future Circular Collider says so explicitly in its own report: "Our objective is not to question the existence of the European Organization for Nuclear Research itself" (39) A.

This is the central result of this file. There is no substantive scientific challenge to the validity of accelerator results. The entire serious critical tradition concedes the physics and attacks the priorities, the theory that motivates the next machine, or the epistemic status of what was learned. A critique of this field that attacks the data is attacking the one joint the critics themselves have never found loose. Any synthesis built on this archive must take that constraint as binding.

The corollary cuts the other way, and is equally Tier A: because almost nobody has attacked validity, the field's internal error-detection record is the only evidence about validity there is - and that record is both better and worse than its public presentation. Better, because the field publishes its own disconfirmations, including a 5-sigma disagreement found in 2024 when someone finally recalculated the tenth-order QED contribution independently (47). Worse, because that recalculation took until 2024 for a number published in 2012 and corrected by 15% in 2018 (49), and the comparison it feeds is now limited by a 5.5-sigma disagreement between two measurements of the fine-structure constant (48).

Inclusion criteria for the count#

The number 60 is a floor, not a census. It counts an entry when all four hold:

  1. A named individual or a named organised body (not "some physicists", not an anonymous blog).
  2. A datable act: testimony, a filing, a published book or paper, a formal report, a signed public letter, or an organised action.
  3. Directed at an accelerator programme or its results - not at string theory alone. (This is why the "string wars" contribute only their accelerator-relevant participants.)
  4. Traceable to a primary document or a peer-reviewed scholarly treatment, not to a press characterisation alone.

What this excludes, and why the real number is larger: unnamed signatories (the 80 Franco-Swiss elected officials count as one entry, not 80); the roughly 500 theory papers written on the 750 GeV excess count as one episode; the 263 national and individual submissions to the 2026 European Strategy update (37) are not individually audited here; Congressional opponents of the SSC other than the two amendment sponsors; every physicist who voted against the SSC inside the American Physical Society's divisions; and the entire pre-1961 period. A full census of category (b) alone would run to hundreds.

What it also excludes: people whose challenge I could not source to a primary document. Several named LHC-safety campaigners appear only on an opposition website (lhc-concern.info) that would not serve to an automated client during this research; they are entered only where a court record or scholarly article names them.

The SSC endgame, with numbers A. Congress was told in 1987 the project could be done for about $4.4-6 billion (2). By 1989 DOE's own estimate was $8.249 billion in 1990 dollars and a DOE-commissioned estimate reached $11.8 billion (1). In February 1993 GAO reported a $630 million overrun on a $1.25 billion construction budget; by March 1993 the public estimate was $8.4 billion, with figures to $11-12 billion in circulation (1, 2). One third of the funding was to be non-federal - $1 billion from Texas and $1.7 billion from abroad, mostly Japan; by 1990 the only foreign pledge was $50 million in in-kind contributions from India (1). A June 1993 draft Inspector General report found inadequate documentation for $203 million, about 40% of money spent to that point, alongside $12,000 for Christmas parties and over $20,000 a year for office plants (1, 2).

The votes A: House 232-181 to terminate, 17 June 1992 (Wikipedia gives 231-181 for the 1992 House vote; Kevles gives 232-181 - unreconciled discrepancy, flagged); House 280-150, 24 June 1993; Senate rejected a killing motion 57-42, 30 September 1993; House 282-143, 19 October 1993, which ended it; Clinton signed the cancellation on 30 October 1993 (1, 2). Spent at cancellation: nearly $2 billion ($400 million-$1 billion from Texas, the rest DOE), 22.5 km of tunnel and 17 shafts complete (1, 2).

The structural point C: particle physicists were about 10% of the American physics community in the 1980s, and the opposition came overwhelmingly from the other 90% - condensed matter, masers, superconductivity (1). This rests on Kevles's demographic figure and on the named opponents' fields. It would be falsified by evidence that the opposition was predominantly from inside high-energy physics; the named record does not support that.

What the courts actually held, and what they did not A. This matters because both sides misreport it. The Hawaii court dismissed for lack of subject-matter jurisdiction: US participation was not a "major Federal action" under NEPA, because the $531 million US contribution to a $5.84 billion machine is under 10%, future budgeted funds "cannot be considered", and under the 1997 International Cooperation Agreement the US holds only non-voting observer status on the CERN Council with no role in financial, policy or management decisions. The court added that NEPA was not enacted to resolve scientific debate: "[t]he political process, and not NEPA, provides the appropriate forum in which to air policy disagreements." It expressly did not reach standing, mootness or the limitations bar (8). The Ninth Circuit affirmed on 24 August 2010 on standing: no injury in fact (only speculative "potential adverse consequences"), no causation or redressability because CERN built and controls the machine and was never properly served. Sheldon Glashow and Frank Wilczek filed a brief saying the doomsday scenarios had no realistic chance (9). No tribunal anywhere has ever adjudicated collider safety on the merits.

Documented human cost of the safety campaign A. The LHC team reported receiving death threats and threatening emails and calls demanding the experiment be stopped (September 2008). On 10 September 2008 a 16-year-old girl in Sarangpur, Madhya Pradesh, died by suicide after doomsday coverage on the Indian channel Aaj Tak; this is reported, and causation is not established (13). Brian Greene answered the question in the New York Times on 11 September 2008 with one word: "no" (13).

D1 Andrew Pickering sociology of science; Edinburgh, then Illinois, then Exeter; PhD in physics (London) and in science studies (Edinburgh) 1984 Constructing Quarks: A Sociological History of Particle Physics. The contingency thesis: a different but comparably successful high-energy physics was possible - as Hacking summarises it, "Not determined by how the world is, not determined by technology now in existence". What the "string wars" was actually about A. George Johnson coined the term at KITP on 20 October 2006. Critics: Smolin, Woit, Glashow, Richter, Lawrence Krauss, Dan Friedan, Reiner Hedrich, with 't Hooft worried about the public nature of the dispute. Defenders: Michael Duff, Polchinski, David Gross, Leonard Susskind, Brian Greene, Gabriele Veneziano, Sean Carroll, Michael Green. The disputes were testability and falsifiability; demarcation (Susskind: scientists, not philosophers, should decide what counts as science); supersymmetry's use as a shield against falsification; the landscape's predictive power; and sociology - whether string theory's dominance over funding, hiring and career paths suppressed alternatives. No winner declared; the controversy receded as string theorists moved into cosmology and QFT (23). It was a dispute about theory. It was not a dispute about whether accelerators work. Anyone citing it as such is misusing it.

Duff's own 2011-12 rebuttal (24) is useful as the defenders' named document. He concedes that "many problems remain to be solved", that M-theory has no smoking-gun prediction, that the landscape problem will not be resolved soon, that criticisms of string theorists as arrogant or unwilling to listen have "some truth", and that he shares concern about pressure to conform, especially in the US. He also writes that "shortly after this was written, funding for the two European research networks on string theory was withdrawn" followed by "Coincidence?" - offered by him as suggestive, not demonstrated (24).

What happened to critics professionally - established from evidence#

The retaliation thesis is not supported by the record. Stated plainly so it cannot be mistaken: I searched specifically for cases in which a scientist lost a post, a grant or a promotion for opposing an accelerator programme, and found none A.

The affirmative evidence runs the other way:

  • James Krumhansl testified against the SSC before the House Science Committee in 1987 while APS President-elect, and served as APS President in 1989-90 (5). A field that punished accelerator critics would not have seated this one at the head of its largest professional society two years after the testimony.
  • Philip Anderson testified against the SSC in 1989, held his Princeton chair until his death in 2020 at 96, and published an extension of the critique in 2001 (3, 6). Zangwill's biography reports he "became a target of physicists' anger" after the cancellation but had little real political influence; the biography's criticism of him concerns late-career imperiousness, not any sanction (6).
  • Bloembergen, Schrieffer, Richter, Glashow, Yang - five Nobel laureates among the critics - suffered nothing attributable.
  • Peter Woit holds a permanent senior lectureship at Columbia mathematics and has published his criticism continuously for twenty years (27). The honest qualification: he had already exited the particle-theory research track before the book.
  • Lee Smolin remained founding faculty at the Perimeter Institute (25).
  • Antonio Ereditato resigned as OPERA spokesperson on 30 March 2012 - the single clearest case of a career cost in this file, and it was for an erroneous result, not for criticism. He then became professor emeritus at Bern, co-founded DUNE, led the ATLAS Bern group during the Higgs discovery, and joined Yale Physics as visiting professor in February 2021 on a three-year joint appointment with Fermilab (45, 55). Dario Autiero resigned as physics coordinator the same day and remained at CNRS (45).
  • Otto Rossler's LHC paper was rejected by two journals and Nicolai said publicly it "would not pass the referee process in a serious journal" (13, 14) - a reputational cost, not an institutional one, and his documented conflicts with Tubingen (1993-2001) predate the LHC entirely (14).

Claims Examined and Not Supported (Tier D)#

D-1. "Accelerator results are fabricated or systematically faked." REJECTED. The prediction of a fabrication thesis is suppression of disconfirmation. The record shows the opposite, repeatedly and recently: Volkov's 2024 independent calculation of the tenth-order QED coefficient was published in Phys. Rev. D reporting a 5-sigma disagreement with the field's only prior value (47); CDF and the three LHC experiments have published mutually contradictory W-mass measurements and said so in print (46); the 750 GeV excess was retired by the same two collaborations that reported it, on their own new data, within eight months (44); OPERA published its own cabling error and its leaders resigned (45); the Particle Data Group prints a standing warning about psychological danger in its own tables (20). A field that fabricates does not generate this paper trail. Rejected.

D-2. Unzicker, "the Higgs discovery is fake and fooled the Nobel committee" (2013). REJECTED. Self-published with no peer review and no response from either collaboration. It does not engage the test that decides the question. Cea (2012), a serious physicist raising a serious alternative interpretation, asked for a spin-parity measurement (50); ATLAS delivered it, excluding spin-2 and 0- at more than 99% confidence on full Run 1 data across three decay channels (51). The resonance was found independently by two collaborations with different detector technologies reporting consistent masses, and the Vietnamese 2015 review - written precisely to ask "Was the Higgs boson discovered?" - concluded the couplings "are compatible with the standard model Higgs boson" (58). A claim of fakery that never addresses the falsifying measurement is not a scientific claim. Rejected.

D-3. Consa, "QED precision rests on fudge factors and manipulated data" (2020). PARTLY SUPPORTED AS HISTORY; REJECTED AS AN INFERENCE OF MANIPULATION. This is the most interesting of the three, and it must be split.

What survives. The historical episodes he assembles are real and were documented inside the field before him. Millikan's discarded oil drops, Einstein and de Haas's g = 1.02 +- 0.10, de Haas's later values of 1.2, 1.11 and 1.55, the clustering of speed-of-light results, the too-tight clustering of the ohm ratio - all appear in Jeng's survey with primary citations (20). Franklin's bandwagon finding in the PDG record is his own field's (20). Consa's report that Kinoshita's team revised the fifth-order coefficient from 7,795 to 6,675 between 2014 and 2018 is correct (49). And his structural worry - that multi-thousand-diagram calculations nobody has independently repeated cannot be trusted - was vindicated in 2024 when Volkov repeated one and disagreed at 5 sigma (47). That much the archive should carry forward.

What fails. The inference from "unchecked" to "manipulated". He offers no new calculation, no re-analysis of raw data, and no documentary evidence of manipulation; his evidence is published revisions, published independent checks and published disagreements - that is, the output of a self-correcting field, not the cover-up of a fraudulent one. And he never addresses the decisive asymmetry: the 2023 electron magnetic moment measurement at 0.13 ppt (48) came after the Laporta (2017) and Kinoshita (2018) theory values were published, and the comparison is now limited not by QED but by a 5.5-sigma disagreement between two independent measurements of alpha (48). A theory that had been quietly tuned to the data could not be bottlenecked by an independent input it was supposedly tuned around. The general claim is rejected; the specific historical record is retained in category (e) and in the hardware/software streams, where it belongs.

D-4. "The Higgs particle never existed - a formal proof of category error" (2025, self-posted). REJECTED AS NOT ASSESSABLE AS SCIENCE. No venue, no peer review, and authorship could not be verified because the host refused automated retrieval. A "formal proof" that an empirically measured resonance of definite mass, spin and parity does not exist is not a testable proposition. Named here so it is not launderable into this archive by omission.

D-5. "Critics of accelerator programmes are punished professionally." NOT SUPPORTED. See the preceding section. Krumhansl was elevated to the APS presidency two years after testifying against the SSC; Anderson kept his chair for three decades; Woit holds a permanent Columbia post; Ereditato went on to DUNE and Yale; Zarnecki and Koppenburg criticise the FCC from posts they retain. The only funding-withdrawal insinuation located is Duff's, it is offered as a coincidence rather than a demonstration, and it alleges harm to the defenders. Falsification condition: this would be overturned by documented cases - a denied tenure case, a cancelled grant with a dated decision letter, a dismissal - tied to accelerator criticism. None was found; the search was not exhaustive.

D-6. "The courts found the LHC safe." NOT SUPPORTED - and the inverse is also not supported. No tribunal has adjudicated collider safety on the merits, anywhere. Hawaii dismissed for want of a "major Federal action" under NEPA and expressly did not reach standing, mootness or limitations (8); the Ninth Circuit affirmed on standing (9); the Bundesverfassungsgericht reviewed the executive's risk assessment only for plausibility and said courts cannot substitute their own scientific judgement (11); the ECtHR refused interim measures by e-mail with no reasons (10); the Zurich court dismissed on CERN's treaty immunity (10). Citing these as judicial vindication of safety misreads them. Citing them as evidence of a cover-up misreads them equally: they are ordinary jurisdictional rulings, and the only court that touched the substance - OVG Munster, Oct 2012 - deferred to the safety reports rather than examining them (12).

D-7. "There is organised scientific opposition to the FCC on safety grounds." NOT SUPPORTED for the current round. The 2023-2026 organised opposition - Co-Cernes (27 associations), Noe21, the 80 Franco-Swiss elected officials - is explicitly environmental, financial and procedural. Noe21 states that its "objective is not to question the existence of the European Organization for Nuclear Research itself" (39), and Joyeux explicitly does not question the scientific interest (41). The safety cohort of category (c) is a 1999-2012 phenomenon and has not reconstituted around the FCC.

D-8. "Philip Anderson opposed the SSC because he thought particle physics was wrong." NOT SUPPORTED. His argument was comparative return on public money - "dollar for dollar" - plus the claim that condensed matter is equally fundamental and the charge that officials misled Congress about spin-offs (1, 4). Wikipedia's own summary of the condensed-matter case concedes the SSC "would do high-quality research" (2). Nothing in the record has Anderson disputing the validity of particle physics results.

D-9. "The string wars show that particle physicists doubt their own machines." NOT SUPPORTED. The dispute was about the testability and institutional dominance of string theory (23). Its leading critics accept collider results explicitly: Woit says it is "extremely difficult to find a flaw in the Standard Model" (26); Richter treats experiment as the way out of the impasse and expects higher-energy machines (22). Transposing the string critique onto the hardware is a category error, and it is the single most common error in secondary writing on this subject.

D-10. "Hossenfelder disputes the LHC's results." NOT SUPPORTED. She states that the 2012 Higgs discovery completed the Standard Model's particle inventory and that the Standard Model "still has some loose ends" (29). Her claim is about expected return on the next machine. Any use of her as a validity critic misrepresents her.

D-11. "The constructivist challenge (Pickering) says accelerator results are wrong." NOT SUPPORTED, and this is the distinction the whole file turns on. Pickering's claim is contingency, not error: that a different and comparably successful high-energy physics was available, and that communal judgement rather than nature alone closed the choices (17). That is a claim about the modal status of the knowledge, not its correctness, and the standard objections to it (Ackermann on the lower plasticity of apparatus, Hacking on the co-evolution of theory and equipment) are objections to the contingency, not defences against a charge of error (17). A critique that cites Pickering as if he showed the quark model false is misusing the strongest category (d) source in the literature.

D-12. The Noe21 / Janot-Grojean carbon gap. UNRESOLVED, NOT A FINDING EITHER WAY. 72 Mt CO2eq (39) versus ~1.6 Mt lifetime (40) is a ~45x disagreement between two published documents with named authors. Neither reconciles with the other. This file records the dispute and the probable drivers (FCC-hh inclusion; EU-mix versus French/Swiss grid factor) as C, and asserts neither figure.
