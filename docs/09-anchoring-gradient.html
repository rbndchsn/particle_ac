The Anchoring Gradient

Whether the external constraint on physics weakens as the stakes rise, whether money and advocacy run the other way, and whether the whole observation is a tautology

What this stream found

  • The gradient is real, but the governing variable is not energy — it is whether anything in use depends on the observable. Energy only correlates because usable cross sections fall with it.
  • The strongest counterexample found, and it is instructive: LHC radiation-to-electronics and machine protection is a genuine external anchor operating at TeV energies — 80 Run-2 beam dumps from radiation faults, FLUKA dose predictions tested against detectors, 362 MJ of stored beam. But its reach scales with cross section, so it anchors inclusive soft production and provably cannot reach the rare-process tail where discovery claims live.
  • The official economic case for the LHC assigns 1.7% of its €16.4bn benefit to scientific knowledge, and 66% to spillover and human capital. Both it and the UK national evaluation monetise the physics bibliometrically.
  • Surviving thesis, narrowed: accountability does not travel with claim-strength.

Examined and struck out

  • That spending runs inversely to anchoring — false, and contradicted by the appropriation table. NNSA Weapons Activities took $19.3bn in FY2025 against High Energy Physics's $1.22bn; Basic Energy Sciences took 2.1× HEP. Inside HEP, the neutrino programme (~$680M) outspends Energy Frontier, Cosmic Frontier and theory combined (~$435M).
  • That unanchored means untested. Electroweak fits predicted the top mass at 140–190 GeV and a light Higgs before measurement; lattice QCD predicted Bc and fD+.
  • That technological spin-offs anchor the physics. MRI magnets, hadron therapy, the Web and grid computing anchor the engineering.
  • That the gradient is unique to particle physics. The same shape appears in cosmology, string theory and deep-time geology.
  • That anchored means known. In the most heavily anchored domain of all, plain density-functional theory underestimates band gaps by around 49%.
NoteThe tautology objection — that this gradient is simply what “frontier” means — is conceded in full for the gradient's existence. What defeats it for the thesis as a whole is that the spending half was empirically falsifiable and was falsified: a tautology cannot be refuted by an appropriation table.

What This Document Tests#

The archive has been circling one structural claim without stating it. 07_epistemic_audit/closed_loop_v1.md ends by rejecting the proposition that the closed loop is equally closed everywhere, on the ground that "its length and the strength of its external anchors vary enormously between a tandem doing backscattering and a collider inferring a short-lived particle." That sentence contains an unexamined thesis. This document examines it.

The claim, stated as sharply as it can be:

The external anchoring of physics is strongest where the stakes are lowest and weakest where they are highest. Low-energy domains are pinned by technology that fails visibly and expensively if the physics is wrong. High-energy domains are anchored progressively less. Beyond-the-Standard-Model searches have no external anchor at all, by construction, because they look for things nothing else in the world interacts with. Meanwhile funding, institutional defence and public justification run in the opposite direction from the anchoring.

That is three separable claims, and they are not equally interesting:

  • A — the gradient exists. External anchoring declines monotonically with energy scale and with distance from applied physics.
  • B — the gradient has a specifiable shape. What remains anchored at high energy is identifiable, and it is not the part that carries discovery claims.
  • C — the opposing gradient exists. Money, personnel, strategy advocacy and public justification increase as anchoring decreases.

Claim A is close to a definitional truth and will be treated as such. Claim B is the one with content. Claim C is empirical, testable against budget documents, and it fails at national scale — a result reported in full below, because a claim that cannot be falsified by a budget table is not worth making and a claim that is falsified by one must be said to be.

What an External Anchor Is#

The definition is the whole argument. A loose definition makes the gradient appear by construction; a tight one risks excluding every case and proving nothing. The definition used here is deliberately tight, and the places where tightening it does the work are flagged.

An external anchor on a physical claim is a constraint satisfying all four of the following.

  1. Foreign origin. The constraint originates outside the discipline that produced the claim — a different discipline, an industry, a regulator, a clinic, or a natural system nobody in the discipline controls.
  2. Costly failure. If the claim is wrong, something in the world fails: a device does not work, a patient is not cured, a clock drifts, a target cracks, a yield is three times the prediction. The failure is visible, expensive and non-negotiable.
  3. Disinterested arbiter. Whoever declares the failure has no stake in the claim's survival. A chip either yields or it does not. A reactor either goes critical where predicted or it does not.
  4. Non-absorbable. The failure cannot be accommodated by adjusting the claim and keeping the practice. A free parameter that soaks up the discrepancy is not an anchor; it is a hinge.

Explicitly not anchors, and these exclusions are where the definition bites:

  • Internal consistency. A theory agreeing with itself.
  • Agreement between two measurements inside the same framework. Two LHC experiments agreeing is not external, however independent their hardware, because the framework, the generators and the correction procedures are shared — this archive has already documented the shared lineage in 04_software_algorithmic (claim C1b, SUPPORTED).
  • Goodness-of-fit. A model fitting data it was tuned on, or data within the range it was tuned over.
  • Novel prediction. Argued at length in §5.6 below. A prediction made before measurement is a severe test in Mayo's sense (6) and is epistemically valuable, but both the predicting and the measuring happen inside the discipline, and the cost of failure is a theory, not a device. Severity and externality are different virtues and the archive must not conflate them.

Classes of anchor. Not all anchors are the same kind, and the distinction matters for the verdict:

Class Kind Failure signature Who pays
E1 Operational / technological a device does not work the manufacturer, the operator, the user
E2 Safety / clinical a patient is harmed, a criticality excursion, a wrong yield the patient, the worker, the public
E3 Metrological / legal a unit, a standard, a trade or regulatory measurement is wrong the measurement system and everyone downstream
E4 Cross-disciplinary natural an independently constrained natural system contradicts the claim nobody — a theory dies, nothing breaks
E0 None the claim's falsity has no consequence outside the discipline nobody

E1–E3 satisfy all four conditions. E4 satisfies conditions 1, 3 and 4 but not condition 2. When helioseismology constrains the solar model, or Oklo constrains the fine structure constant, or non-observation of proton decay kills minimal SU(5), the constraint is genuinely foreign and genuinely non-negotiable — but nothing in the world fails. No device stops, no patient is harmed, no money is lost. E4 constraints are epistemically strong and institutionally weak: they do not impose a cost on being wrong, and the claim under test here is ultimately about where the cost of being wrong lands. Calling E4 a "foreign constraint" rather than an "external anchor" is this document's most contestable move, and §5.7 argues it both ways.

Where the Definition Could Be Contested, and What Turns On It#

Four honest edge cases.

The transfer problem. An anchor constrains the specific physical content a device uses, not the whole framework in which that content is expressed [C — rests on the case analyses in §3; falsified by one device whose operation depends on a high-energy observable]. GPS anchors relativistic time dilation and the caesium hyperfine transition. It does not anchor QED at two loops, let alone QCD at a TeV. Someone defending the field can say the framework — relativistic quantum field theory, gauge invariance, Lorentz invariance — is anchored by every working device. The reply is that the framework is anchored only in the sense that nothing built on it has failed, and nothing built on it uses its high-energy content. This is the weakest form of support a claim can have while still having some.

Adequacy, not truth. An anchor forces the physics to be right enough for the purpose; it does not force it to be true. Semiconductor manufacture is the cleanest demonstration (§3.3). This cuts both ways: it weakens the inference "anchored, therefore known," and it strengthens the structural point, because what an anchor actually disciplines is accountability, not correctness.

Timing. An anchor can arrive late. Nuclear physics was unanchored in 1930 and anchored by 1945. A detected BSM particle could be exploited a century hence. So "no anchor" is a statement about now, never about in principle — with the single exception argued in §3.8.

Self-service. The definition was written by someone looking for a gradient. Condition 2 is the one doing the work: relax it and the gradient flattens considerably, because E4 constraints are abundant at the high end. The defence is that condition 2 is the condition the original claim rested on — "a device that does not work, a patient who is not cured, a clock that drifts" — and that §5.7 reports what happens when it is relaxed rather than hiding it.

On the regress. The definition is a practical answer to Collins's experimenter's regress (2): you cannot know an instrument works without knowing the right answer, and you cannot know the right answer without a working instrument. Franklin's response is a list of strategies — calibration against known inputs, independent confirmation, testing rivals' methods on one's own data, intervention — and his central condition is that "calibration of the apparatus is independent of the phenomenon one wants to observe" (3). Perović's reply, specifically about the LHC, is that in-situ calibrating and measurement procedures and parameters there are "closely and systematically interrelated," so empiricists should "question their insistence on the independence of calibration from the outcomes of the experiment" (4) [B — Franklin and Perović are the named advocates; Perović does not conclude that the LHC is open to the regress, only that the independence assumption is too crude]. Hacking's entity realism — manipulative success as grounds for belief (1) — is the strongest available version of the anchoring intuition, and E1 is its operational form.

The Gradient, Domain by Domain#

Atomic Physics and Low-Energy QED#

Anchor class: E1 + E3. Failure latency: microseconds to hours. The strongest anchor in physics.

The second is defined by a caesium hyperfine transition, which makes atomic physics the only domain on this list whose correctness is a matter of law rather than of opinion A. Optical clocks now report systematic uncertainties of 1.3 × 10⁻¹⁸ (7), and two clocks of different species have been compared at 2.08 × 10⁻¹⁸ over a 1,350 km fibre link (8); an international roadmap toward redefining the second on an optical transition is in progress (9) A.

The anchoring is not the precision — precision alone is internal. The anchoring is that the timing chain is load-bearing for systems with no interest in atomic physics. On 26 January 2016, after a decommissioned satellite was removed from the GPS constellation, the UTC offset broadcast on some legacy signals was wrong by 13 microseconds (10, 11) A. One telecommunications operator logged over 2,000 alarms; another generated nearly 2,500, with roughly 40 network elements entering holdover because backup inputs were missing; about two-thirds of one operator's GPS timing estate was affected; roughly 30% of another's units caused downstream switches to enter free run with no visible alarms; clearing one customer's alarms took about four hours two days later (10) A.

Thirteen microseconds. Not a theory in dispute — an operational event, diagnosed by telecoms engineers, at a cost measured in engineer-hours and network risk. That is condition 2 and condition 3 in a single incident.

What is anchored: transition frequencies, relativistic time dilation and gravitational redshift, systematic shift budgets (blackbody, collisional, Zeeman), and the whole metrological chain. What is not: QED's loop structure. The electron anomalous magnetic moment agrees with theory at the part-per-trillion level, which is the most impressive number in physics, and it is an internal agreement — a calculation and a measurement, both inside the discipline, with no device downstream.

Nuclear Physics#

Anchor class: E2 + E1. Failure latency: milliseconds to months. The most consequential anchor.

The single clearest case in the whole gradient is Castle Bravo, 1 March 1954. The predicted yield was 6 megatons. The device yielded about 15, roughly 250% above prediction, because the designers assumed only one of the two lithium isotopes in the fusion fuel would be reactive and both were (12) A. The consequences: fallout over inhabited Marshall Islands atolls after a wind shift, evacuation and long-term health monitoring with serious long-term effects among islanders; a Japanese fishing vessel sailing into the plume, returning with a sick crew, radioactive tuna and one dead fisherman; a diplomatic crisis with Japan; and the first public acknowledgement of multi-megaton capability (12) A.

A nuclear cross section was wrong. People died, islands were contaminated, and a government had to explain itself. Conditions 1 through 4, without qualification.

The institutional response is the anchoring regime that now exists. Evaluated nuclear data libraries are validated against an international benchmark set of critical and subcritical configurations — the ICSBEP Handbook — maintained outside any single laboratory (13), and the libraries are revised against it (14) A. The stockpile has been certified annually since 1992 without explosive testing, which makes simulation the load-bearing element of a weapons-certification decision (17) A. In inertial confinement, the National Ignition Facility missed its ignition goal for a decade after 2009 before achieving target gain above unity in December 2022 — a public, expensive, dated failure of a high-energy-density prediction, followed by a public, dated success (18) A.

Two honest qualifications.

Tokaimura was not a physics failure. The 1999 criticality accident killed two workers, exposed 24 others to up to 48 mSv, evacuated about 161 people and produced an estimated 13 billion yen liability against a 1 billion yen insurance limit — and it was caused by a procedural change made three years earlier without regulatory permission, workers tipping uranyl nitrate from stainless steel buckets into a precipitation tank that bypassed the criticality-safe geometry, with no understanding of the risk at 18.8% enrichment (16) A. The IAEA attributed it to "human error and serious breaches of safety principles." The criticality physics was right; the practice was not. An anchor disciplines the system, and most of what it catches is procedure, not theory. This weakens any simple reading of "anchored, therefore the physics is known."

The anchored domain corrects itself under measurement, and the correction propagates upward. Measured reactor antineutrino fluxes at short baselines sit about 6% below the Huber+Mueller prediction, with a world-average ratio of 0.943 ± 0.008 (experimental) ± 0.023 (model), and several experiments see a localised 4–6 MeV excess at about 4.4σ local significance in Daya Bay (15) A. Daya Bay's fuel-evolution measurement found the predicted yield per fission for ²³⁵U significantly too high while ²³⁹Pu agreed, disfavouring a uniform deficit at 2.8σ (15) A. The conservative reading is that the nuclear data were wrong, not that there is a sterile neutrino (15) [B — the sterile interpretation retains advocates and global fits exist that favour revised ²³⁵U plus a sterile state]. Note the direction of travel: a discrepancy in a weakly-anchored domain (neutrino oscillation phenomenology) was most plausibly resolved by a correction in a strongly-anchored one (fission beta spectra). The gradient has a slope, and information runs down it.

Solid State and Semiconductors#

Anchor class: E1. Failure latency: one process run. The most economically brutal anchor — and the one that proves anchoring is not truth.

Here the gradient claim meets its most instructive complication. Semiconductor manufacture is the most unforgiving E1 anchor in existence: a wrong dopant profile is a dead wafer lot, and the cost is immediate and attributable. And yet the first-principles physics used in the domain is known to be wrong by large factors.

Plain semilocal density functional theory underestimates semiconductor band gaps by roughly half: a benchmark of 56 compounds gives PBEsol a mean absolute error of 1.99 eV and a mean percentage error of −48.8%. Hybrid functionals (HSE06) reduce this to about 0.78 eV MAE and −14.7% mean percentage error, and G₀W₀ to about 0.57 eV MAE and −12.5% — still "a noticeable underestimation of the band gaps by about 14% on average" (19) A.

A 14–50% error in the most basic electronic property of the material, in a domain where errors cost millions per lot. The resolution is that production does not use first-principles band structure to set process parameters. It uses process simulators calibrated against measured profiles — TCAD calibration of ultra-shallow junctions against SIMS data is a documented industrial practice (20) A. Implantation range distributions are computed with Monte Carlo codes whose parameters are fitted over the ion, energy and substrate ranges in use.

The lesson, and it governs the rest of this document: an E1 anchor does not force the physics to be true. It forces the predictive pipeline to be adequate over the operating range, and it permits — indeed rewards — empirical calibration that absorbs theoretical error. What the anchor guarantees is not knowledge. It is accountability: somebody finds out, fast, at a cost they cannot argue with, and must change something [C — rests on the band-gap and TCAD facts above; falsified by a production process whose parameters are set from uncalibrated first-principles calculation].

This is why the gradient matters even though anchoring is not truth. The claim under test is not "high-energy physics is wrong." It is that the mechanism which forces anyone to find out and pay for it weakens as the stakes rise.

Accelerator Engineering — and the Distinction That Decides Everything#

Anchor class: E1, strong. Scope: the machine, not the physics done with it.

Accelerator physics is anchored as hard as semiconductor physics and the failures are on the public record. Strong focusing (21) is the enabling theoretical result of the whole field and it works. But the interesting material is where predictions failed.

At the LHC [A, all from (22)]: the nominal design peak luminosity was 1.0 × 10³⁴ cm⁻²s⁻¹ and the machine routinely exceeds twice that, with 2.2 × 10³⁴ tabulated as achieved against 1.0 × 10³⁴ design, and 60 events per crossing against 27 nominal. Design energy is 7 TeV; running has been at 3.5, 4, 6.5 and 6.8 TeV, limited by magnet retraining. Sectors trained more slowly than expected and lost training memory after thermal cycles, which the operations literature calls a surprise; sector 23 needed 54 extra quench events to reach 6.8 TeV, each recovery costing 8–12 hours. Arc heat loads ran significantly above what impedance and synchrotron radiation predict, attributed mainly to higher secondary electron yield from surface chemistry changes, to the point that post-LS2 degradation makes the HL-LHC nominal configuration incompatible without surface treatment in LS3; cryogenic capacity is about 10 kW per arc against an 8 kW design. Dust-induced beam losses ("UFOs") were the main cause of beam-induced quenches in Runs 2 and 3. The original integrated-luminosity design goal was 300 fb⁻¹; Runs 1 and 2 delivered about 160 fb⁻¹ and 2024 alone delivered 122.2 fb⁻¹ to ATLAS and CMS.

At LEP, stray currents from trains running between Geneva Cornavin and French destinations leaked into the earth and returned through the LEP tunnel, using the vacuum chamber as a conductor, interacting with the dipole field and raising the beam energy by up to 12 MeV over a 14-hour fill (23) A.

At the Spallation Neutron Source, the mercury target vessel's calculated lifetime was about 5,000 MWh. Two vessels failed back-to-back in 2014 after about 100 and about 600 MWh. None of the 11 targets used since 2006 reached the predicted lifetime. Each cost about $1 million; the failures plus an unrelated fault cost 1,000 hours of operating time, about half recovered by rescheduling, with net available operating time for the fiscal year down about 10%. The cause was not fully identified: both failed at welds, one at the same weld that failed in 2012–13, with cyclic thermal and mechanical fatigue suspected (24) A.

These are genuine, documented, expensive failures of prediction in a domain where GeV protons meet matter. Which is exactly why the next sentence is the hinge of this document.

These anchors constrain accelerator physics, not particle physics [C — rests on the content of the predictions that failed; falsified by showing that a machine performance prediction depends on a rare-process observable]. The physics in a dynamic aperture calculation, a secondary electron yield, a weld fatigue model or a stray-current field perturbation is classical and relativistic electrodynamics, materials science and thermomechanics. Whether the collisions at the interaction point are correctly interpreted as Higgs production is logically independent of whether the magnets quench where predicted. A machine that reaches design luminosity has demonstrated that its builders understand beams. It has demonstrated nothing about what the detectors' inferences mean.

The one serious exception is energy deposition from the collisions themselves, which is a hadronic high-energy observable and which does fail visibly. It is treated as the document's strongest counterexample in §5.2.

Hadron Physics and QCD#

Anchor class: E1 and E2 at the low-GeV end, thinning rapidly with energy. The genuine middle of the gradient.

QCD is where the gradient stops being a step function and becomes a slope.

What is internal. The lattice calculation of the light hadron spectrum from first principles (25) is one of the field's genuine achievements and it is entirely internal — a calculation reproducing measured masses. The lattice has also made pre-measurement predictions that could have failed: the B_c mass was calculated as 6304 ± 12 (+18, −0) MeV and later measured by CDF at 6287 ± 5 MeV, agreeing at slightly over 1σ, with the lattice paper posted before the measurement was announced; f_D+ was calculated as 201 ± 3 ± 17 MeV and CLEO-c announced 223 ± 17 ± 3 MeV a few days later (26) A. These are severe tests in Mayo's sense (6). They are not external anchors: the predicting and measuring communities overlap, and failure would have cost a lattice action, not a device.

What is external, and this is real. Three independent E1/E2 anchors reach into hadronic physics:

  • Hadron therapy. Carbon-ion treatment planning depends on nuclear fragmentation cross sections, and the models are measurably imperfect. Against reference data, Geant4 overestimates the total inelastic ¹²C + ²⁷Al cross section by about 5–10%; at 300 MeV/u the beryllium partial cross section worsened by roughly 10–15% between releases while boron improved to under 20% model difference; and the authors' recommendation for hadrontherapy is a specific physics list, QGSP_BIC_HP with the Opt4 electromagnetic constructor (27) A. Proton range uncertainty propagates directly into clinical margins (70) A. Proton therapy centres grew from 52 in 2015 to 128 by early 2026 (61) A. A wrong fragmentation cross section is a wrong dose to a real patient: condition 2, condition 3.
  • Atmospheric-neutron single event effects. Soft error rates in microelectronics from atmospheric neutrons are standardised and measured by industry (29), and the models rest on proton- and neutron-induced nuclear reaction cross sections up to the GeV range (30) A. The arbiter is a data centre or an avionics bay.
  • Muon radiography. Cosmic-ray muon flux and transport models are load-bearing for imaging that has located a large void in Khufu's pyramid (31) and produced three-dimensional images of the interior of a damaged reactor at Fukushima Daiichi (32) A.

Medical isotope production belongs here too: yields depend on charged-particle reaction cross sections, and the IAEA runs coordinated data projects precisely because wrong data means no isotope reaches a clinic (33) A.

The energy ceiling of the anchor. Every one of these operates at or below roughly a GeV per nucleon. Above that, the external constraint thins to energy deposition and shielding — which is where the counterexample in §5.2 lives — and the character of what is anchored changes from exclusive to inclusive.

Electroweak Precision#

Anchor class: E0, with one E4 constraint. The first domain with no device downstream.

Nothing in the world fails if the electroweak global fit is wrong. No clock drifts, no patient is harmed, no wafer is scrapped. What pins electroweak precision is the fit's own internal consistency across many observables, plus two things worth separating.

Severe internal tests, which are real. Global analyses constrained the top mass to roughly 140–190 GeV before its 1995 discovery at the Tevatron, against a present value near 172.9 GeV; early fits favoured a light Higgs of order 100 GeV rather than order 1 TeV, and later fits made successful predictions of M_H before the 2012 observation at 125.1 GeV (34) A. These were risky and they could have failed. They are the best evidence in the archive that a domain can be unanchored and still well-tested. §5.6 argues both ways on whether they count.

One E4 constraint. Big Bang nucleosynthesis bounded the number of light neutrino species from primordial abundances before LEP measured the Z invisible width (38) A. A foreign discipline constrained an electroweak parameter and agreed. Nothing broke when it did.

The diagnostic case. In 2022 CDF reported the W mass as 80,433.5 ± 9.4 MeV, seven standard deviations above a Standard Model prediction of 80,353 ± 6 MeV; CMS in 2024 reported 80,360.2 ± 9.9 MeV, consistent with the prediction and with previous LHC results (35) A. A 7σ outlier against both theory and the rest of the field was adjudicated over years by more measurements of the same kind, by the same discipline. The muon anomalous magnetic moment tells the same story from the other side: the Fermilab experiment reached 127 ppb and the world average 124 ppb (36), while the Standard Model prediction moved under a reassessment of the hadronic vacuum polarisation in which lattice and data-driven determinations disagreed (37) [B — the lattice/R-ratio dispute has named advocates and the 2025 white paper's resolution is itself a theory-side adjudication].

In an E1 domain the arbiter is the device and the timescale is a production run. Here the arbiter is the discipline and the timescale is a decade. That difference, not any claim about correctness, is the gradient.

The Higgs Sector#

Anchor class: E0. No anchor exists outside the collider programme.

The question in the brief is whether there is any anchor outside the collider programme. The answer is no, and the field says so itself. Asked in 2012 what the Higgs boson would be used for, Sean Carroll justified the expense "by the purely intellectual reward associated with understanding the missing piece of the Standard Model," noted the historical pattern that applications follow curiosity-driven work, and invited readers to speculate about what a cheap tabletop Higgs would be good for — offering no application himself (39) A.

There is no metrological role: nothing in the SI depends on the Higgs. No clinical role. No device. The Higgs couples to mass, and everything that interacts with the Higgs field already does so whether or not anyone understands the mechanism, which is precisely why the mechanism's correctness has no operational consequence. Vacuum stability, electroweak phase transition cosmology and the hierarchy problem are constraints from other theory, not from the world.

This is not a criticism of the Higgs discovery, which is the best-tested piece of the last fifty years of collider physics. It is a statement about where the cost of being wrong would have landed: nowhere outside the discipline.

Beyond-the-Standard-Model Searches#

Anchor class: E0, and uniquely, E0 by construction rather than by circumstance.

This is the only place in the document where "no anchor" can be argued in principle and not merely as a matter of present fact.

The argument: an E1 anchor requires that something in use depends on the phenomenon. A BSM particle is defined by a coupling small enough to have escaped every existing measurement. Any particle feeble enough to be undiscovered is, by that same feebleness, too feeble for anything to depend on. The absence of an external anchor on an undetected BSM particle is therefore not contingent; it is entailed by the search condition [C — rests on the definition of the search region; falsified by a technology whose operation requires a not-yet-discovered particle, which would be self-contradictory]. The qualifier is temporal: anchoring becomes available after detection, never before. Nuclear physics in 1930 was in exactly this position.

The state of the searches A:

  • WIMP direct detection. LZ's 4.2 tonne-year exposure reports no evidence for an excess over expected backgrounds, with a best-fit WIMP count of zero at every mass tested from 9 GeV to 100 TeV, and a world-leading spin-independent limit of 2.2 × 10⁻⁴⁸ cm² at 40 GeV (40). The irreducible background is now coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering from solar and atmospheric neutrinos (40), and XENONnT has reported a first indication of solar ⁸B neutrinos via that channel (41) — the detectors have become neutrino instruments on the way to their limit.
  • Extra dimensions. Limits only; no signal (42).
  • Supersymmetry. Simplified-model bounds reach about 2.4 TeV for first- and second-generation gluinos and squarks, 1.3 TeV for stops and sbottoms, 1 TeV for electroweakinos — and the interpretation is contested: in the pMSSM, gluinos near 1 TeV and stops near 400 GeV remain viable, and a named body of work (Baer, Barger and Savoy on practical naturalness; Arkani-Hamed and Dimopoulos and Giudice and Romanino on split SUSY; Hall and Nomura on high-scale SUSY) holds that the principle is not excluded and that "no currently feasible collider can definitely exclude SUSY" (43) [B — this is the clearest Tier B in the document, with advocates named in the source].

Note what the last item shows about condition 4. The exclusion of SUSY parameter space is real work and it has cost the theory something. But the response available to the theory — move to a corner of parameter space the bound does not cover — is exactly the absorption that condition 4 excludes. An E1 anchor cannot be answered that way. A wafer does not yield in a different corner of parameter space.

Astrophysical and Cosmological Particle Claims#

Anchor class: E4 only — and E4 here is at its strongest anywhere in physics.

The archive must not understate this. Astrophysics and cosmology have killed particle-physics theories, and this is the only part of the high-energy landscape where any outside agency has ever done so.

  • Minimal SU(5) is dead, killed from outside the accelerator programme. Georgi-Glashow minimal SU(5) predicted a proton lifetime of about 10³⁰–10³¹ years. IMB set a limit of 5.5 × 10³² years, Kamiokande 2.6 × 10³², and the present Super-Kamiokande limit on p → e⁺π⁰ is 2.4 × 10³⁴ years at 90% CL — the model is ruled out, by a detector in a mine, by non-observation (44) A.
  • The solar neutrino problem was a conflict between a particle-physics assumption and an astrophysical model, and the astrophysics won. Helioseismology independently constrained the solar interior, closing the escape route that the Sun was wrong (45), and the resolution was that neutrinos have mass (46) A. This is the single best case in the archive of a foreign discipline overturning a particle-physics assumption.
  • SN 1987A bounds axions and other weakly coupled particles by the cooling argument, and those bounds constrain parameter space no terrestrial experiment reaches (47) A.
  • Oklo constrains variation in fundamental constants through the burn-up record of a natural reactor that operated two billion years ago (48) A.
  • Neutrino mass is now bounded directly at below 0.45 eV by KATRIN (49) and indirectly, more tightly and more model-dependently, by cosmology A.
  • Dark energy is the weakest link: the preference for evolving dark energy over a cosmological constant in recent survey data is actively contested on statistical grounds (50) B.

Every one of these satisfies conditions 1, 3 and 4. None satisfies condition 2. When minimal SU(5) died, nothing stopped working and nobody paid. That is why they sit in E4 and why §5.7 treats the boundary as the document's most contestable line.

The published cost-benefit analysis of the LHC decomposes total benefits of €16.4bn against €13.5bn of costs, giving a net present value of €2.9bn and a 92% probability that benefits exceed costs, as follows (58) A:

The field's own economic appraisal of its flagship instrument assigns 1.7% of the benefit to the physics and 66.1% to technology transfer and training A. The quasi-option value of future discoveries is set to zero and applied-research benefits are excluded (58).

The critique of that appraisal is as important as the numbers. Scientific output is valued at the salaries of the researchers who produced it and citations at reading time — so a discovery's value is made proportional to the time and wages spent on it; publication and citation counts are unreliable because erroneous papers attract citations and per-author metrics are meaningless in multi-thousand-author collaborations; only 10% of CERN's general administrative costs are charged to the LHC, and at 75% the total would rise by several billion euros, which the author notes is an uncertainty roughly the size of the NPV itself; and the conclusion is that the method "cannot really evaluate the benefit of new scientific knowledge," nor does any other current method do so quantitatively (58) A.

The UK national evaluation shows the same structure independently. UK subscription was about £144m in 2019, 16% of Member State subscriptions, with total UK investment averaging about £152m a year over the decade; about 500 UK firms won roughly £183.3m in CERN contracts over 2009–18, with surveyed suppliers self-reporting an average +4% turnover boost extrapolated to about £1.05bn of additional turnover; young UK researchers who engaged with CERN earn an estimated 12% more, worth about £489m in wages over the decade; public willingness to pay is estimated at about £1.2bn, extrapolated from a French survey. Knowledge is valued at over £495m "based on UK authorship and citation of CERN papers" (59) A.

Both official appraisals value the physics bibliometrically — by authorship, citation and researcher salary — and the technology by procurement and wages. In other words, at the unanchored end of the gradient, the quantified justification for the instrument is composed almost entirely of things that are not the physics claims, and the one component that is the physics claims is measured by an index internal to the discipline A.

That is the defensible form of claim C. Not "money runs opposite to anchoring" — the budget tables refute that. But: as external anchoring falls, the justification for expenditure shifts from the physics to proxies (spillover, training, prestige, existence value), and the physics component is assessed by discipline-internal metrics [C — rests on (58) and (59); falsified by an official appraisal of a major collider that monetises the physics knowledge by an external measure and finds it dominant].

The Demand Asymmetry#

A sharper way to put the same point, using user counts. Basic Energy Sciences user facilities reported about 11,606 users in FY2021 — 7,617 at light sources, 685 at neutron scattering facilities, 3,304 at nanoscale science research centres — having peaked near 12,000 at the light sources alone in FY2014 (53) A.

That is comparable in magnitude to the LHC user community. The difference is composition. BES facility users are overwhelmingly from outside the discipline that built the facility: chemists, structural biologists, materials engineers, industrial crystallographers whose own careers fail if the beamline misreports. LHC users are the discipline. Anchored facilities are funded by external demand; unanchored facilities are funded by internal advocacy [C — rests on the composition of the two user bases; falsified by showing a substantial non-particle-physics user base whose work depends on collider data]. This is the structural asymmetry the money tables fail to capture, and it is why the money tables refuting claim C do not dispose of the thesis.

Attempts to Break the Claim#

This is the section the document exists for. Each candidate counterexample was run down to the question: does it anchor the particle physics claims, or only the engineering?

Spin-offs, One at a Time#

Hadron therapy. The strongest of the medical cases. CERN hosted the 1996–2000 PIMMS study, which produced the optimised proton and carbon-ion synchrotron design behind CNAO in Pavia and the basis for MedAustron, and an ultra-compact 750 MHz RFQ built on Linac 4 experience accelerates protons to 5 MeV in about 2 m, licensed to a spin-off (60) A. Patients are treated; a wrong dose harms them. Verdict: a genuine E2 anchor — on Bethe stopping power, nuclear fragmentation cross sections below about 400 MeV/u, and accelerator engineering. Not on TeV-scale particle physics. The physics it tests is the physics of §3.2 and §3.5, which the gradient already places at the anchored end.

MRI superconducting magnets. This is the most-repeated claim in the public case for colliders, and it is weaker than its reputation. CERN's own review of its impact on medical technology does not claim MRI magnets at all; its superconducting magnet work is for ion-therapy facilities, including a toroidal gantry design, and its magnetic-field-related medical product is a handheld radiation survey meter usable near PET-MRI scanners — explicitly "not an MRI magnet" (60) [A/B — the broader industrial claim that accelerator demand built the NbTi wire industry is real but is a claim about superconductor manufacturing capacity, not about physics]. Verdict: anchors materials and magnet engineering. Not particle physics.

The World Wide Web. Anchors nothing physical. It was written at a particle physics laboratory; its success is independent of whether any particle physics claim is true. The UK evaluation's citation of the Web as adding 2.9% to global GDP is a global figure offered as context (59) A. Verdict: no anchor.

Grid and distributed computing. Engineering. Verdict: no anchor.

Detector technology in imaging. Medipix and Timepix hybrid pixel chips derive from LHC tracking detectors; Medipix3 sorts photons into energy bins, enabling spectral X-ray imaging, and a MARS Bioimaging scanner using Medipix3 produced the first 3D colour X-ray images of human extremities in 2018; Crystal Clear crystals feed time-of-flight PET; Charpak's multiwire proportional chamber, invented at CERN in 1968, led to a radiography technology used in orthopaedics (60) A. Verdict: a real E1/E2 anchor on detector engineering and on photon-matter interaction at keV energies. The clinical images do not depend on any TeV-scale claim being true.

A general note on this whole family. Every spin-off anchors the layer of physics the device uses, which is always lower-energy than the physics the laboratory was built to study. That is not an accident of this sample; it follows from the fact that a device must operate at energies and cross sections where effects are large enough to exploit [C — rests on the case-by-case verdicts above; falsified by one deployed device whose operation depends on a TeV-scale exclusive observable].

The Strongest Counterexample: Radiation to Electronics and Machine Protection#

This is the one that nearly breaks the claim, and it deserves to be stated at full strength before it is qualified.

At the LHC, the physics of the collisions themselves produces a radiation field in the tunnel that destroys electronics, and the cost is counted in machine availability. Over Run 2, radiation-to-electronics faults caused 80 beam dumps: 24 in 2015, 8 in 2016, 14 in 2017 and 34 in 2018, attributed to power converters (35), the Quench Protection System (28), magnet circuits (10), RF (4) and other systems (3). Each dump costs about three hours to re-establish stable beams with collisions. The dump rate stayed below 0.5 per fb⁻¹ except in 2015. Total ionising dose measured by the RadMon system at IP1 in 2017 and 2018 is reported as "in good agreement with the predictions," which are FLUKA Monte Carlo simulations (62) A. Separately, the machine carries about 362 MJ of stored beam energy at nominal LHC design and about 677 MJ at HL-LHC nominal (22) A, and machine protection depends on getting energy deposition right.

This satisfies all four conditions. The constraint is foreign to the physics analysis (it is enforced by accelerator operations and equipment groups), the failure is visible and expensive (lost beam time, destroyed hardware, in the limit a damaged magnet), the arbiter is disinterested (the availability record), and it cannot be absorbed by a parameter.

And it is an anchor on high-energy hadronic physics. The radiation field in the tunnel is produced by TeV-scale proton-proton collisions. If inelastic particle production at those energies were badly mis-modelled, the shielding and the equipment placement would be wrong, and the machine would be unavailable in ways its operators could not explain.

The qualification, which is the crux of this document. What this anchor constrains is inclusive, bulk, high-cross-section observables: minimum-bias multiplicity, forward particle flux, shower development and energy deposition. It cannot constrain the rare-process tail, for a reason that is structural rather than practical: a process rare enough to require a discovery claim is, by that same rarity, far too rare to deposit a measurable dose. The anchor's reach is proportional to cross section, and discovery physics lives where the cross section is smallest [C — rests on the proportionality of dose to cross section and on the R2E facts in (62); falsified by a machine-protection or shielding calculation whose outcome depends materially on a rare-process cross section].

Supporting evidence on how accurate the anchored part actually is, which is more interesting than either extreme would predict: against ATLAS calorimeter beam tests at 20–160 GeV, both Geant4 and the Geant4-FLUKA.CERN combination overestimate the pion-to-electron response ratio by "up to a few percent"; Geant4 with FTFP_BERT reproduces the roughly 10% response difference between pion and proton showers in TileCal at 16–30 GeV; and on longitudinal shower length the two codes disagree in opposite directions, Geant4 underestimating and FLUKA.CERN overestimating, especially above 120 GeV (28) A. So at the anchored frontier of high-energy hadronic physics the accuracy is percent-level on bulk response and the two leading codes do not agree with each other on shape. That is precisely what a narrow anchor looks like: tight where the money is, loose immediately outside it.

And the SNS targets (§3.4) show what happens when a GeV-scale prediction in this same family is tested hard: eleven targets, not one reaching the calculated 5,000 MWh lifetime, two failing at about 100 and 600 MWh, cause not fully identified (24) A. The anchor fired, the prediction lost, and the field could not immediately say why.

The Spallation and Soft-Error Family#

Treated in §3.5 and §5.2; the verdict is recorded here for completeness. Atmospheric-neutron single event effects (29, 30), medical isotope production yields (33) and muon radiography (31, 32) are genuine E1/E2 anchors reaching up to roughly a GeV. They anchor nuclear reaction and transport physics. They do not reach the electroweak or Higgs sectors at all, and they reach QCD only in its inclusive, low-energy regime.

Did Any High-Energy Result Ever Get Overturned by an External Constraint?#

Searched for specifically. No case was found in which an E1–E3 anchor overturned a particle-physics claim [C — an absence of evidence, stated as such; falsified by one documented case, which would be a major result and should be sought actively]. The nearest candidates and why each falls short:

  • OPERA's superluminal neutrino claim of 2011. Retracted after instrumental faults were identified in the timing chain. The contradicting evidence was a theoretical argument from vacuum-Cherenkov-type energy loss (64) and a measurement by another detector in the same tunnel (63) A. Both internal. Nothing in the world failed; the claim failed.
  • Minimal SU(5). Killed by proton-decay non-observation (44) — a genuine foreign constraint, but E4.
  • The reactor antineutrino anomaly. The closest thing to the anchored domain correcting the less-anchored one, and it runs downhill: nuclear fission data correcting neutrino phenomenology (15) [A/B]. Still not a device failing.
  • Castle Bravo and the SNS targets. Real anchored failures of prediction — in nuclear and accelerator physics, not in particle physics (12, 24) A.
  • The pentaquark Θ⁺ and the CDF W mass. Both corrected, both internally (35, and the Θ⁺ case recorded in 05_publication_governance) A.

The honest summary: the field self-corrects, repeatedly and on the record, and in no documented case has the correcting agent been something outside it that stopped working.

Precision Electroweak Prediction as an Anchor — Argued Both Ways#

The brief asks whether pre-measurement predictions of the W and Z masses, the top mass from loop corrections, and the Higgs mass range count as anchors. The question deserves both answers in full.

The case that they do count. These predictions were risky and specific. The top mass was constrained to roughly 140–190 GeV before the particle existed as an observation, and came in near 172.9 GeV; fits favoured a light Higgs of order 100 GeV against the then-live alternative of order 1 TeV, and the boson arrived at 125.1 GeV (34) A. On Mayo's severity criterion, a test is severe when the hypothesis would probably have failed it if false (6), and these qualify. Worrall's use-novelty criterion is also satisfied: the top and Higgs masses were not used to construct the theory being tested (5). Under predictivism in any of its versions — Maher's reliable-method argument, Worrall's conditional-versus-unconditional confirmation, Hitchcock and Sober's overfitting guard (5) — this is strong confirmation, and much stronger than "the model fits." A critic who dismisses it is not being rigorous; they are being hostile.

The case that they do not count, which is this document's position. Three reasons, each tied to a condition.

Condition 1, foreign origin, fails. The predicting and the measuring were done by one discipline with overlapping personnel, shared software lineage, shared systematic-uncertainty conventions and shared correction procedures. This archive has documented the shared lineage quantitatively (claims C1a′, C1b, SUPPORTED, in 04_software_algorithmic and 08_evidential_audit). An agreement between two parts of one community is not a foreign constraint, however severe it is.

Condition 2, costly failure, fails. Had the top mass come in at 400 GeV, the cost would have been borne by a theory and by some careers. No clock would have drifted and no patient would have been harmed. There is no price signal.

Condition 4, non-absorbability, fails. The electroweak framework has designed-in latitude for exactly this contingency: S, T and U parameters, and now effective-field-theory operators, exist to parameterise deviations. A discrepancy becomes a measurement of a new coefficient. That is the correct scientific response and it is also precisely the absorption condition 4 excludes.

Resolution. Severe internal testing and external anchoring are distinct virtues and electroweak physics has the first in abundance and the second not at all. The archive must therefore never write "unanchored" and mean "untested." Making that slide would be the single fastest way to discredit the defensible material here, and §8 records it as a rejected claim (D2). What the distinction buys is a sharper question than "is it true?": who finds out, how fast, and at whose expense? In the electroweak sector the answer is the discipline, over a decade, at its own expense — as the W mass episode demonstrates in detail (35).

Neutrino Physics Through Solar and Atmospheric Observation — the Hardest Case#

The brief asks whether neutrino physics is anchored through astrophysical cross-checks produced outside accelerator laboratories. This is the document's most contestable line and both readings are stated.

The case for calling it anchored. The solar neutrino problem was resolved against a particle-physics assumption. The Sun's interior was independently constrained by helioseismology, which removed the option of blaming the astrophysical model (45); detectors in mines, built and operated by a community that was not the accelerator community, measured a deficit; and the resolution was that neutrinos have mass (46) A. Proton decay non-observation killed minimal SU(5) outright (44). Big Bang nucleosynthesis counted neutrino species before LEP did (38). SN 1987A bounds axion couplings no laboratory reaches (47). Oklo bounds constant variation over two billion years (48). These constraints are foreign in origin, disinterested, and non-absorbable — three of four conditions, and the three that matter most epistemically. On any ordinary usage, neutrino physics is externally constrained, and more so than any other part of high-energy physics.

The case for calling it a foreign constraint rather than an anchor. Condition 2 fails in every case. When minimal SU(5) died, nothing stopped working. No manufacturer lost a lot, no patient was mistreated, no operator logged 2,000 alarms. The arbiter was disinterested but also unharmed, and an arbiter who bears no cost imposes no cost. The claim under test is about where the price of error lands, and E4 constraints send no invoice.

Verdict. Neutrino physics occupies a genuine intermediate class — E4-strong, E1-absent — and it is the reason the gradient is a slope and not a cliff. Calling it unanchored full stop would be false. Calling it anchored in the sense that semiconductor physics is anchored would also be false. The archive should carry both facts: neutrino physics has the strongest foreign constraints and none of the operational ones, and the funding data (§6.2) show it is also the best-funded part of US high energy physics, which is a point against claim C and is recorded as such.

The Tautology Objection#

The strongest counterargument to this entire document, stated without softening:

The gradient is simply what "frontier" means. A frontier is by definition the region where nothing yet built depends on the phenomenon, because if something depended on it, it would be applied physics. You cannot have external technological anchors for phenomena nobody can yet exploit. So "external anchoring is weakest at the frontier" is true the way "the newest thing is the least established thing" is true — a definition wearing the clothes of a critique. Worse, it is a critique that would have condemned nuclear physics in 1930, semiconductor physics in 1945 and laser cooling in 1975, each of which later became the anchored domain the argument now uses as its standard.

This objection is correct about claim A and must be conceded in full. The existence of the gradient is near-tautological and is not presented here as a finding. The historical point is also conceded: every anchored domain in §3 was unanchored once, and the document's own §2 edge case on timing says so. Anyone reading claim A as a criticism of high-energy physics has read it wrong.

Three things survive the objection, and they are the document's actual content.

First, the opposing gradient is empirical and it was falsified. A tautology cannot be refuted by an appropriation table. Claim C was tested against DOE appropriations and failed: the most anchored science is the best funded, by a factor of 16 (51), and inside HEP the best-funded line is the sub-domain with the strongest foreign constraints (52). A thesis whose second half can be — and was — destroyed by budget records is not a tautology. It is a conjunction of a near-definitional observation with a contingent empirical claim, and the empirical claim lost. That result is worth more than the thesis would have been if it had won.

Second, the shape of the surviving anchor is not tautological. That TeV-scale physics retains a narrow E1 anchor on inclusive soft production through radiation-to-electronics and machine protection (62), that this anchor's reach is proportional to cross section and therefore cannot extend to discovery physics, and that at its own frontier the two leading simulation codes disagree in opposite directions on shower shape (28) — none of that follows from the meaning of "frontier." It is a specific, checkable claim about which observables are disciplined by cost and which are not, and it is falsifiable by a single counterexample (§5.2).

Third, what the justification documents count is not tautological. That the official cost-benefit analysis of the LHC assigns 1.7% of benefit to scientific knowledge and 66% to spillover and human capital (58), and that both it and the UK national evaluation monetise the physics by authorship and citation (58, 59), is a documented property of the appraisal literature. A field could have been anchored only weakly and still have justified itself primarily by its physics. These ones do not.

Does the claim survive? Partly, and not in the form it was stated. As stated — "anchoring is weakest where the stakes are highest, and funding runs the other way" — it does not survive: the first half is near-definitional and the second half is false at national scale. What survives is narrower and sharper:

Accountability does not travel with claim-strength. As external anchoring falls, the entity that determines whether a claim was wrong converges on the entity that made it; the anchoring that remains at the high-energy end covers bulk inclusive observables and, for structural reasons tied to cross section, cannot cover the rare-process claims that carry discovery weight; and the quantified public justification for instruments at that end rests on proxies for the physics rather than on the physics, with the physics component itself assessed by discipline-internal metrics.

That formulation is testable, partly documented, partly inferential, and it is not a tautology. It is also considerably less dramatic than the claim this document was asked to test, which is the usual outcome when a structural intuition is made to meet budget tables and primary literature.

Claims Examined and Not Supported#

D1 — That weak external anchoring implies a claim is false, fabricated, or probably wrong. Rejected D. There is no inferential route from "nothing outside the discipline would break if this were wrong" to "this is wrong." The Higgs discovery, the electroweak fit and the measured exclusion of large volumes of SUSY parameter space are unanchored in this document's sense and also, as far as any evidence shows, correct. This archive's binding scope boundary already excludes fabrication claims (00_method/research_frame_v1.md) and nothing here reopens them.

D2 — That unanchored means unjustified, or untested. Rejected D, and this is the most important rejection in the document. Electroweak physics predicted the top mass in a 140–190 GeV window before the particle was observed and favoured a light Higgs against a heavy one before 125 GeV was measured (34). Those are severe tests on Mayo's own criterion (6) and satisfy use-novelty (5). Lattice QCD predicted the B_c mass and f_D+ before measurement (26). A domain can be unanchored and rigorously tested, and conflating the two would discredit everything defensible in this file. §5.6 argues the point at length precisely so that it cannot be read any other way.

D3 — That this gradient is unique to particle physics. Rejected D. Tested honestly against three other frontier fields.

Cosmology. The same gradient, steeper. Nothing in the world depends on dark energy's equation of state; the preference for an evolving one over a cosmological constant is contested on statistical grounds (50) B. Cosmology's constraints are E4 throughout, and its appeal to a disinterested arbiter is weaker than neutrino physics's, because its independent probes share systematics and a single sky.

String theory. E0 everywhere, and the field has produced an explicit methodological response rather than denying the problem: a sustained argument over whether a research programme with no risky prediction can be assessed non-empirically at all, conducted in the open between the programme's methodologists and its critics (67) [B — the dispute has named advocates on both sides and is not resolved]. The gradient's endpoint is therefore occupied not by particle physics but by a neighbouring field that has openly argued for changing the standard of evidence in response.

Deep-time geology. This one is instructive because it is anchored at its shallow end and not at its deep end — the same shape, driven by the same mechanism. Radiocarbon is calibrated against dendrochronology and tied to absolutely dated cosmogenic spikes in tree rings at 774 and 993 CE (68, 69) A, and basin and petroleum-system modelling is paid for in dry wells, which is as clean an E1 anchor as semiconductor yield (70-adjacent industrial practice) C. Hadean geology has no such anchor at all. Nobody drills for the Hadean.

Conclusion: the gradient is a general feature of frontier science, not a pathology of particle physics. Particle physics is distinctive only in the ratio of expenditure to anchor at the unanchored end — a CHF 15bn instrument (55) against an E0 anchor class — and even that distinction is partly beaten by cosmology per unit of anchor, and entirely beaten by string theory on anchor though not on cost.

D4 — That funding, facility cost and institutional defence run inversely to external anchoring. Rejected D, with numbers. NNSA Weapons Activities, the most heavily anchored physics in the United States, received $19.293bn in FY2025 against High Energy Physics's $1.225bn, and the FY2027 request widens the ratio from about 16 to about 24; Basic Energy Sciences received about 2.1 times HEP (51) A. Inside HEP, the neutrino programme — the sub-domain with the strongest foreign constraints — is the largest single investment, at roughly $680M against about $435M for Energy Frontier, Cosmic Frontier and theory combined (52) A. The narrower claim that survives concerns the composition of the justification, not the magnitude of the spend (A3), and the demand asymmetry between externally-populated and internally-populated user communities (§6.6) is an inference, not a measurement.

D5 — That accelerator engineering's anchors extend to the particle physics done with the machines. Rejected D, but with a carved-out exception that must travel with the rejection. Beam optics, magnet training, electron-cloud heat loads, stray-current field perturbations and target fatigue are anchored hard and have demonstrably failed against prediction (22, 23, 24) A, and none of that bears on the interpretation of collision data. The exception is radiation-to-electronics and machine protection (62), which genuinely anchor inclusive high-energy hadronic production (A2). Stating the rejection without the exception would misrepresent the evidence.

D6 — That spin-offs anchor the physics. Rejected D, case by case (§5.1). Hadron therapy, Medipix, PET crystals, the multiwire chamber, the Web, grid computing and superconducting magnet technology each anchor either engineering or a layer of physics below the one the laboratory was built to study. CERN's own review of its medical impact does not even claim MRI magnets (60) A, which is worth noting given how often the public argument does.

D7 — That BSM searches' lack of an external anchor is a defect of those searches, or a reason not to fund them. Rejected D. An undetected particle's feebleness is the search condition; anchoring is structurally unavailable before detection, not withheld (§3.8). The honest reading is that BSM searches must be justified on grounds other than anchoring, which is what the field does, and the archive's question is whether those grounds are stated candidly — a question for 02_theory_critique and 05_publication_governance, not for this file.

D8 — That novel prediction is an external anchor. Rejected D as definitional (§5.6, A7). Recorded here so it is not re-litigated: predictive success is a severe test and a real epistemic virtue, and it is not what condition 2 asks for.

D9 — That the LHC has no external anchor at all. Rejected D. 80 radiation-induced beam dumps in Run 2, FLUKA dose predictions tested against RadMon measurements, 362 MJ of stored beam requiring correct energy deposition (62, 22) A. The LHC is externally anchored on bulk inclusive observables. The claim is about the reach of that anchor, not its existence, and overstating it would have been the easiest error in this document to make.

D10 — That the claim as originally stated is a finding of this archive. Rejected D. It is near-tautological in its first half (§7) and false in its second (D4). Only the narrowed formulation at the end of §7 and the register entries A1–A4, A7 and A8 may be carried forward.

D11 — That the anchored domains are therefore "known" in a way the unanchored ones are not. Rejected D, and this is the subtlest of the rejections. Plain DFT underestimates semiconductor band gaps by about 49%, and even G₀W₀ by about 14% on average (19) A, in the most ruthlessly anchored domain in science. Eleven of eleven SNS targets missed their calculated lifetime (24). Castle Bravo was off by 250% (12). What an anchor produces is not correctness but accountability under cost — somebody finds out quickly, at a price they cannot argue with, and must change something. The gradient is a gradient of accountability, and saying otherwise would make the archive claim far more than its evidence supports.
