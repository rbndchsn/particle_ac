What this stream found

  • Electron g/2 = 1.001159652180(13) — 0.13 parts per trillion, 15 significant figures, matched to ~1 part in 10¹² by a 12,672-diagram five-loop calculation. This is the most precisely confirmed prediction in science.
  • Calibration point that matters: the test is limited by the imported value of α, and the two best determinations differ from the g/2 value by 2.1σ and 3.9σ.
  • The inferential chain does not turn model-dependent at calibration — ATLAS jets are calibrated to particle level at 0.3% at 300 GeV, anchored on test-beam and in-data balance.
  • It turns model-dependent at two later steps: the object-to-entity mapping (jet→parton, missing energy→invisible particle), and the acceptance factor computed under the hypothesis being tested.
  • Genuinely alive: axions, SUSY as a framework, composite Higgs, heavy sterile neutrinos. Dead or untestable: technicolor, preons, ADD, MOND-as-replacement, eV sterile neutrinos.
  • Real unexplained anomalies: the gallium deficit (>5σ), dark energy tension (3.1–4.2σ), neutrino-mass cosmology tension, strong CP, baryon asymmetry.

Examined and struck out

  • Plasma cosmology, the electric universe, “quarks don’t exist”, Santilli, and all fabrication claims.
  • Unzicker’s falsity claims — his auditability point survives; nothing else does.
  • That the muon g-2 anomaly is resolved — it is displaced, not resolved; the two determinations remain incompatible at ~3σ.
  • That R(K) is a live anomaly — it dissolved, with a published cause.

What this stream is for, and why it must cut both ways#

This file exists to calibrate the rest of the archive. Its job is not to find weakness. Its job is to find the location of weakness, which requires first establishing, with real numbers, where there is none.

Two failure modes are equally fatal here. The first is to understate the Standard Model's empirical standing, which would make every other file in this archive dismissible in a sentence. The second is to accept the field's own framing of its inferential chain without examining it, which would make the archive pointless. The sections below are written so that a hostile specialist and a hostile sceptic would both find something they did not want to read.

Three structural findings, stated up front:

  1. The precision is real and, in places, understated in popular accounts. The Z boson mass is known to 2.2 parts in 100,000. Nine hadron masses were computed from three inputs to 1-3%. These are not interpretive claims.
  2. The inferential chain is robustly instrumental up to the calibrated physics object and becomes irreducibly model-dependent at exactly two later steps - the mapping from object to theoretical entity, and the acceptance factor that converts a yield into a rate. Naming those two steps precisely is worth more than a general complaint about theory-ladenness.
  3. The field's own literature contains the sharpest available statements of its epistemic limits. Any critique that claims the field is unaware is refuted by that literature, and should instead ask whether the acknowledgement is assembled anywhere, by anyone, into an auditable whole.

Part 1 - What is confirmed, and to what precision#

The electron anomalous magnetic moment: the strongest single test, with a caveat the field states openly#

The measurement is g/2 = 1.001 159 652 180 59(13), a fractional uncertainty of 0.13 parts per trillion, which is fifteen significant figures (1). A The Standard Model prediction, dominated by QED, agrees with it at about one part in 10^12 (1). A

The theory side is a five-loop calculation. The tenth-order QED contribution involves 12,672 Feynman diagrams in 32 gauge-invariant subsets (2). A The predicted value is a_e = 1 159 652 181.606(11)(12)(229) x 10^-12, where the three uncertainties are tenth-order QED (0.011), hadronic (0.012), and the input value of the fine structure constant (0.229) (2). A

That last number is the whole epistemic story of this test, and it cuts against the popular framing. The comparison is not limited by QED. It is limited by alpha, which must be imported from an independent measurement, and the two best independent determinations of alpha disagree with each other. The alpha inferred from g/2 plus the SM differs by 2.1 sigma from the Paris rubidium determination and 3.9 sigma from the Berkeley caesium determination (1). A Until that is resolved, predicted and measured values agree only to about delta(g/2) = 0.7 x 10^-12 (1). A

A second calibrating detail: the two independent calculations of the tenth-order QED coefficient disagreed at about 5 sigma until 2024-2025, when the discrepancy was traced to 98 diagrams sharing a common structure whose Monte Carlo integration errors accumulated non-randomly; rerunning those with higher statistics resolved it (3). A The theory side of the most precise test in physics required an independent recomputation to find an error. That is the system working, and it is also evidence that a single calculation at this depth is not self-certifying.

The wider alpha picture is a strong independent argument for the framework: alpha^-1 is determined to 10^-9 from the electron g-2, to 10^-8 from rubidium atom recoil, and to 10^-8 from the quantum Hall effect, by apparatus with nothing physical in common, and the values agree to within about 10 parts in 10^9 (4). A

The muon anomalous magnetic moment#

Fermilab's final result, Runs 1-6, is a_mu = 116 592 070.5(14.8) x 10^-11, a precision of 127 ppb, about four times better than Brookhaven E821 (5)(8). A The experimental world average is 116 592 071.5(14.5) x 10^-11, 124 ppb (5). A

The 2025 Theory Initiative White Paper gives a_mu^SM = 116 592 033(62) x 10^-11, a relative uncertainty of 530 ppb, and a difference from experiment of 38(63) x 10^-11, about 0.6 sigma (6)(7). A

The theory precision is four times worse than the experimental precision. That asymmetry is the live issue, and it is treated in Part 5.

Lattice QCD: the strongest answer to any claim that quarks are bookkeeping#

The Budapest-Marseille-Wuppertal calculation took as input only the light quark mass, the strange quark mass, and the gauge coupling, fixed by the pion and kaon masses plus one scale, and then predicted the rho, K, N, Lambda, Sigma, Delta, Sigma, Xi and Omega masses - about nine genuine predictions - to within 1-3% of experiment (24). Three numbers in, nine out, at percent level. Any account in which quarks are a calculational fiction has to explain this result, and no such account attempts to.

Plain statement#

The Standard Model's confirmed sector is the most precisely tested theoretical structure in the history of science, and the margin is not close. A fifteen-significant-figure measurement matched by a 12,672-diagram calculation; a particle mass known to two parts in 100,000; nine hadron masses predicted from three inputs; a 15-parameter global fit with p = 0.34. A Any critique of this field that does not begin by conceding this is not a critique; it is an error. Everything in the remainder of this file is a claim about what surrounds that core - its motivation, its inferential plumbing, its auditability, and the frameworks that have been proposed to extend it.

Part 2 - The inferential chain, step by step#

The chain is: electronic signal -> reconstructed hit -> track or cluster -> physics object -> theoretical entity, with a rate or cross-section at the end.

Where it is not model-dependent, and why that matters#

Steps one to three are instrumental and anchored outside the theory under test. The ATLAS jet energy scale is a useful worked example precisely because it is the step most often alleged to be circular.

The first calibration step is simulation-based, and the collaboration says so: the baseline correction is MC-derived and corrects for the calorimeter's lower hadron response, energy outside the jet, and dead material (27). A But the target of that correction is stated explicitly: it ensures that "the reconstructed jet energy equals, on average, the energy of the corresponding jet formed by stable particles" (27). A The field calibrates jets to particle level, not parton level. The simulation is then corrected against data by in-situ techniques - pT balance in Z/gamma+jet events, dijet eta-intercalibration - and against single-particle test-beam response via a deconvolution method (27). A The resulting combined uncertainty is 0.3% at pT = 300 GeV and 0.6% at 4 TeV for anti-kT R = 0.4 jets at |eta| < 0.8 (27). A

Generator dependence is present and is treated as an uncertainty rather than hidden: "Jet fragmentation effects are addressed by comparing the results obtained with the Pythia MC event generator" against Sherpa (27). A

A 0.3% energy scale anchored on test-beam single-particle response and in-data momentum balance is not a theory-laden artefact. Calibration against independently established quantities is the oldest item on Franklin's list of epistemological strategies, and this is a textbook instance of it (33). A

Where it becomes irreducibly model-dependent: two specific steps#

Step 4 -> 5, object to entity. This is the real hinge, and it has two signature cases.

Jets as proxies for partons. The calibrated object is a four-vector defined by an algorithm over particle-level energy flow. Calling it "a quark" or "a gluon" is a mapping licensed by QCD factorisation, not a measurement. The field's own strongest statement of the underlying fact is PDG's: "Experiments have produced no evidence for free propagating quarks" (25). A QCD predicts exactly this - quarks are confined in hadrons or deconfined in quark-gluon plasma - and PDG notes the one partial exception, that the top quark decays while still effectively free because its lifetime is shorter than the hadronisation time (25). A

The honest formulation: the parton is never the object of a measurement; the particle-level jet is. Unfolding from particle level to parton level is a further, separately labelled, model-dependent step, and the field performs it as such. Stated that way the claim is correct and survives specialist reading. Stated as "jets are just assumed to be quarks" it is false, because it ignores the particle-level calibration target.

What would falsify the strong version of the critique: a confinement-free formulation of hadronic physics that reproduced Bjorken scaling, R = 11/3, and the lattice hadron spectrum. None exists. C

Missing transverse energy as inference of invisible particles. ATLAS defines pT^miss as the magnitude of the negative vector sum over reconstructed objects and calls it, in its own words, "a proxy for momentum carried by undetected particles" (28). A It is built from hard terms (electrons, photons, hadronic taus, muons, jets) and a soft term consisting of hard-scatter vertex tracks not assigned to any hard object - which means neutral soft energy is simply absent from the construction (28). A

The soft term is where the theory input is explicit: "The uncertainty in the soft term is assumed to be dominated by how well it is modelled by Monte Carlo simulation," and the uncertainty is defined as the maximal data-MC difference across three Z->ll generators (28). A Fake pT^miss arises from mismeasurement, resolution effects and pile-up, and pile-up jets can pass the jet-vertex tagger when a muon track inflates their hard-scatter score, whose "stochastic nature degrades the pT^miss response and resolution" (28). A

So: the imbalance is measured, with quantified resolution, improved up to 30% at high pile-up by tighter working points (28). The interpretation of the imbalance as an invisible particle is a model-licensed step, and the generator-spread-defined soft-term uncertainty is a case where a theoretical prior sets the size of an experimental error bar. Both halves of that sentence are true and the second is the defensible critical claim.

Step 5 -> rate, the acceptance factor. Antoniou's analysis of the LHCb R_K measurement is the clearest published account of the full chain: detection and reconstruction; triggering, which cuts the rate from roughly 13 MHz to about 12.5 kHz and discards most data irretrievably; cut-based selection guided by theoretical knowledge of the decays and backgrounds; efficiency calculation by simulation, corrected by tag-and-probe on calibration data; maximum-likelihood fits to invariant mass distributions; and uncertainty assignment with systematics estimated by varying models and parameters (29). A

His two load-bearing conclusions: "raw data in their pure form are practically useless for the comparison of experimental results with theory," and real and simulated data are combined into the final data model and cannot easily be separated (29). A Theory enters at three levels - general physical principles, the physics of particle-detector interaction, and the specific aims of the experiment (29). A

The efficiency factor is the step that cannot be purified. A yield becomes a branching fraction only by division by an acceptance computed under a hypothesis about the signal's kinematics. When the hypothesis is the thing being tested, the circularity is real; the field's mitigations are blind analysis and explicit uncertainty inclusion, which Antoniou judges to make the theory-ladenness non-vicious rather than absent (29). A

The trigger step is prior-laden and irreversible, and is treated in this archive's 03_hardware_systems/trigger_data_destruction_v1.md; it is not re-litigated here.

Resonance peaks as inference of particle existence#

The sharpest available case study is one the field produced itself. The 750 GeV diphoton excess was reported in December 2015 at 3.9 sigma local (ATLAS) and 3.4 sigma local (CMS), which after accounting for the mass range searched fell to 2.1 sigma and 1.6 sigma global (30)(31). A About 500 theoretical papers appeared between the December 2015 and August 2016 results (31). A More data showed it was a fluctuation, and a Bayesian analysis that found "substantial" evidence for it on the 2015 data became evidence against it with 2016 data included (31). A

Hooper's reading is the one to carry forward: declaring a discovery is a social judgement, not a mechanical threshold, and the textbook view that 5 sigma equals discovery "does not hold up to historical scrutiny" (30). A He notes DAMA's annual modulation quoted at 8.9 sigma is not regarded as a dark matter detection, and argues the LHC's search volume justifies a trials factor near 10^4, which would make a 750-GeV-like anomaly roughly a 5% occurrence (30). A His contrast with the Higgs is explicit: both ATLAS and CMS reported about 5 sigma in 2012 and it was immediately treated as a discovery, which he attributes mainly to the strong prior expectation that the LHC would find a Higgs-like boson, an expectation with no analogue at 750 GeV (30). A

This cuts both ways and must be reported that way. The prior-dependence of discovery declaration is real and a named physicist in the field says so in print. And the 750 GeV episode is simultaneously the best evidence available that the system is not fabricating results: the field published a bump, 500 theorists built on it, and the collaborations killed it with more data.

How much of "we observed particle X" is observation#

Maettig and Stoeltzner's questionnaire and interview study of LHC physicists found that after the 2012 announcement physicists first treated the object as "a" Higgs boson, and that calling it the SM Higgs required precision property measurements that were then still marginal (32). A They also note the 5 sigma threshold is explicitly conventional - "By convention in particle physics" - with different thresholds for evidence, observation and exclusion (32). A They nonetheless defend the discovery as a crucial experiment in an appropriate sense, arguing that locating the LHC within the tradition of earlier precision experiments keeps Duhemian underdetermination at bay (32). A

A defensible decomposition of "we observed the Higgs boson," as of 2026:

  • Observed: an excess of events at 125.11 +/- 0.11 GeV in diphoton and four-lepton invariant mass, in two independently designed detectors, reproduced in five decay channels at 10% precision (18)(19). A
  • Inferred with high confidence: that the excess is a single narrow scalar resonance coupling to W, Z, t, b and tau with SM-proportional strengths, kappa_V = 1.002 +/- 0.027 (20). A
  • Assumed, or at best weakly constrained: that its potential has the SM shape. The self-coupling is bounded only to -1.2 < kappa_lambda < 7.5 (21). A The statement "the Higgs mechanism as described in the SM is confirmed" is therefore stronger than the data on the single parameter that defines the mechanism's shape. C It rests on the self-coupling and charm-coupling bounds above and would be falsified by an HL-LHC self-coupling measurement landing on kappa_lambda = 1.

The field's own statement that its results are published model-dependently#

The LHC reinterpretation report is explicit: "Reinterpretation is only possible with the provision of detailed analysis information by the experiments"; two-dimensional exclusion lines are not useful for reinterpretation because they encode one model's assumptions; when combined results are shown "the relative contribution of each topology or final state is fixed and cannot be varied"; and some analyses, especially ML-based ones, cannot currently be reproduced outside the collaborations (37). A Simplified models cannot capture the full phenomenology of UV-complete models, and when a signal spreads across many channels the derived limits "tend to be highly conservative and often far too weak" (37). A

This is the single most important Tier A fact in the audit. The published form of an LHC exclusion is a statement about a simplified model, not about nature, and the community that produces them says so in a joint report with a list of what would have to be published for the results to be reusable.

Part 3 - Theory-ladenness of observation: strongest version, strongest reply#

The strongest version of the argument#

The classical form is Hanson's claim "that seeing is a theory laden undertaking," extended by later writers to instrument-generated data such as thermometer readings (34). A Kuhn and Feyerabend sharpened it: observations and experimental results are already part of a theoretical framework, so they cannot confirm that framework independently, and no theory-neutral observation language exists (33)(34). A Kuhn drew on the Bruner and Postman card experiment, in which subjects "saw" a black four of hearts as red (34). A

Applied to this field, the strong version has three distinct limbs, and they are not equally strong.

  1. Pickering's contingency thesis. High-energy physics "could have developed in... a nonquarky way" and been equally successful; Hacking's summary is that the outcome was "not fully predetermined" by the world, technology, or social practice (33). A
  2. Galison's selection thesis. Theory determines what counts as a real effect and what counts as background; his muon example shows how the Oppenheimer-Carlson calculation changed which phenomenon was treated as unexplained (33). A This is the limb that transfers most directly to trigger menus and analysis cuts.
  3. Antoniou's inseparability thesis, the strongest contemporary version because it is derived from a current experiment rather than a historical case: real and simulated data are combined into the final data model and cannot easily be separated (29). A Morrison reinforces it from the other side: the Higgs results logically depend on simulations used in data analysis and causally depend on simulations used to design the detectors (35). A

Limb 3 is where a serious critique of this field should live. Limb 2 is correct and bounded. Limb 1 is the weakest, and should not be leaned on.

The strongest reply#

  • Data versus phenomena. Bogen and Woodward: raw data are idiosyncratic and shaped by irregular causal factors, so theories rarely predict individual data points; investigators use data to infer facts about phenomena, which are regular enough for systematic prediction, and theories are tested against those (34). A This dissolves much of the force of "observation is theory-laden," because the test is not against observation reports.
  • Assuming a theory is not always vicious. Laymon argues the Michelson-Morley experiments did not depend on the theory under test; Woodward argues the same for Millikan's oil drop experiment (34). A
  • Theory-ladenness comes in degrees. Karaca's position, cited in both SEP entries, is that theory-ladenness varies across different parts of a theory - which converts the issue from a blanket objection into an empirical question to be settled case by case (33). A
  • Robustness and intervention. Hacking: observations stay robust across changes in apparatus theory, and intervention strengthens belief in apparatus and observation together (33). A Galison concedes that instruments can keep the same readings after a theory change (33). A
  • The world constrains outcomes. The standard rationalist reply to Pickering is that no successful theory of light could yield a different speed of light; Weinberg's remark is that "no mountaineer would ever name a book Constructing Everest" (33). A
  • Morrison, who establishes the dependence, is not a sceptic about the result, partly because test runs of the apparatus gave data for an independent check on the simulations (35). A
  • The decisive empirical reply. Three inputs produce nine hadron masses to 1-3% (24); R = 11/3 rather than 11/9 supplies the factor of three for colour (26); alpha^-1 agrees to 10^-9-10^-7 across caesium recoil, rubidium recoil, the quantum Hall effect and the electron g-2 (4). A contingency thesis has to explain that convergence, and no version of it attempts to. C Falsified by: a demonstration that any of these agreements is an artefact of shared calibration, which would require shared physical dependence between apparatus that have none.

Where this leaves the audit#

The defensible position is the one both SEP entries converge on: theory-ladenness in this field is pervasive, acknowledged, mostly correctable, and concentrated in identifiable steps - acceptance and efficiency factors, trigger and selection priors, and the object-to-entity mapping. The indefensible position is that it is global and vitiating. The useful critical question is not "is observation theory-laden" but "is the theory-ladenness accounted for in the uncertainty budget, and are the correlations between experiments that share generators and PDFs treated correctly" - which is a question with an answer, and which the reinterpretation report (37) and Junk and Lyons (36) both show the field has only partially addressed. C

Part 4 - Competing frameworks, status-rated#

Ratings: alive (viable, testable, actively constrained), constrained (surviving only in corners of parameter space, or surviving at the cost of its original motivation), effectively dead (its characteristic predictions are excluded), untestable (no foreseeable experiment discriminates).

Technicolor (original, QCD-like) - EFFECTIVELY DEAD#

Claims: electroweak symmetry breaking by a new strong gauge interaction, with no elementary Higgs; condensing technifermions give W and Z their masses; extended technicolor gives quarks and leptons theirs (38).

Tests and outcomes: the S parameter estimated in QCD-like technicolor is "significantly greater than the experimentally allowed value"; ETC interactions induce mu->e gamma and K_L->mu e unless Lambda_ETC exceeds roughly 1000 TeV, or 10^4 TeV with CP violation; and technipions are then at most a few GeV, conflicting with LEP searches at the Z (38). A The 125 GeV scalar is the finishing blow - technicolor does not generically predict a boson that light, and "the original models are largely ruled out" (38). A

Do not present this as a live contender.

Composite Higgs / walking and conformal technicolor / strong-dynamics EWSB - CONSTRAINED#

Plasma cosmology (Alfven, Lerner) - REJECTED on five independent quantitative grounds#

Wright's point-by-point refutation supplies the arithmetic (55). A

  1. CMB spectrum. Lerner's fit has two effectively free parameters; it was a passable fit to the 1994 FIRAS points but is about 6 standard deviations from the improved Fixsen et al. (1996) data, and refitting changes chi^2 by only 0.7 for two new degrees of freedom - worse than an average random model. Current spectral-distortion limits are about 100 times tighter than when the model was written.
  2. Helium versus metals. Observations show about 3.2 g of helium per 1 g of heavier elements. Producing the Sun's 28% helium by stellar nucleosynthesis would yield about four times the observed 2% heavy-element fraction; producing 23% helium alongside the ~0.01% metals of old halo stars is "completely out of the question."
  3. Deuterium and lithium. Cosmic rays show about 80 deuterium nuclei per lithium nucleus; the Universe has about 6 million. Spallation origin for lithium leaves about 99.99% of the observed deuterium unexplained.
  4. No absorbing curtain. The model requires >99% radio absorption toward Cygnus A, which is not seen; Abell 2218 shows no CMB hole with a Sunyaev-Zeldovich deficit under 1 mK; 3CRR sources at 0.4 < z < 0.5 would appear as about -2 mK holes in Saskatoon data and none was seen; and a density peak at Virgo would make absorption strongly direction-dependent whereas radio sources are uniform to within a few percent.

Also recorded: electromagnetic forces are about 10^17 times too small to affect the Sun's galactic orbit (55). A

"Quarks do not exist" - REJECTED, with the specific evidence a sceptic must address#

The claim takes two forms: that quarks are mathematical bookkeeping with no referent, and that confinement is an unfalsifiable excuse for their non-observation. The evidence against both (26) A:

  • Point-like constituents. SLAC deep inelastic scattering: counting rates at large momentum transfer far above elastic predictions, and F_2 depending only on x and not on Q^2 at fixed x = 0.25. Bjorken scaling means the constituents cannot be resolved as extended objects.
  • Spin one-half. Neutrino scattering: a (1-y)^2 angular distribution for antineutrinos against a flat one for neutrinos; and sigma/sigma-bar about 2 rather than the naive 3, which requires a sea antiquark fraction of roughly 20%.
  • Fractional charge. Dividing the electron data by 5/18 brings the neutrino and electron F_2 data into agreement - a quantitative prediction of the fractional-charge assignment with no free parameter.
  • Momentum not carried by quarks. Average momentum fraction x-bar ~ 0.43 implies about half the nucleon momentum is carried by something else, which the model names as gluons and which was then found.
  • Three colours. R = sigma(hadrons)/sigma(mu mu) above the b-bbar threshold would be 11/9 without colour; the measured value is 11/3, a factor of exactly three. Independently, W leptonic branching fractions of 11% each close to 100% only if each quark flavour comes in three colours.
  • Gluon spin. TASSO determined J = 1 for the gluon from three-jet angular correlations.
  • Ab initio spectrum. Nine hadron masses from three inputs to 1-3% (24).
  • One quark seen before hadronisation. PDG notes the top quark decays while still effectively free because its lifetime is shorter than the hadronisation time (25).

On confinement: PDG states "Experiments have produced no evidence for free propagating quarks," and that QCD predicts exactly this (25). A The honest caveat, which should be recorded rather than suppressed: confinement has no rigorous proof. Yang-Mills existence and the mass gap remains an unsolved Clay Millennium Problem, and the Clay statement is that experiments and computer simulations suggest a mass gap exists but "no proof of this property is known" (62). A That is a genuine gap in the mathematical foundations. It is not evidence that quarks are unreal, because the absence of a proof of a mechanism does not weaken the nine quantitative results above - which is what any sceptic has to defeat, and what none has attempted.

A representative example of the claim in circulation is Santilli's general-physics preprint arguing that both quarks and neutrinos are untenable conjectures, with the neutrino rejected "due to lack of energy" in beta decay and replaced by an "etherino" (58). REJECTED. Neutrinos have been directly detected for seventy years, their flavour oscillations measured to 6-15% (23), their solar fluxes measured species by species including the CNO cycle (56), and their absolute mass bounded at 0.45 eV (49). An argument that the neutrino does not exist is not a competing hypothesis; it is a claim contradicted by direct detection in multiple independent experiments.

"Collider results are fabricated or circular artefacts" - REJECTED#

The specific mechanisms that defeat this, with their honest limits.

Against fabrication and circularity: blind analysis, with procedures fixed before the signal region is viewed and precision measurements using a hidden offset removed only at unblinding (36) A; internal working-group review and staged approval (36) A; multiple detectors of independent design, with high-profile results announced by competing collaborations (36) A; calibration anchored on quantities established elsewhere - single-particle test-beam response and in-data pT balance for the jet energy scale (27), and a 0.3% energy scale as the result A; published likelihoods on HEPData, Rivet routines, and preservation efforts (36) A; and a 5 sigma threshold with pre-registered criteria (36) A.

The strongest single piece of counter-evidence is the record itself. The 750 GeV excess was published, built on by about 500 papers, and then killed by the same collaborations with more data (31). The R(K) anomaly was published at 3.1 sigma and then removed by the same collaboration reanalysing the same data with better electron identification and background modelling, with the reason published (59). A fabricating system does not do either of these things.

The honest limits, which the field states: reproduction only checks computation, and a flawed analysis "contains the same flaws" when reproduced; replications using overlapping data are not independent, and shared systematic errors make them less independent than they appear; unrecognised systematics - "unknown unknowns" - are the most problematic; exclusion limits carry a built-in false-exclusion rate (36). A

So the residue of the claim, after the fabrication part is discarded, is a genuine and bounded one: correlated systematics across experiments that share event generators, parton distribution functions and detector-simulation frameworks are not obviously treated correctly, and no external party is positioned to check. That is a Tier C structural inference resting on (36) and (37); it would be falsified by a published cross-experiment correlation treatment for generator and PDF systematics. It is a far weaker claim than fabrication and a far stronger one, because it might be true.

2. "The electron g-2 is a clean twelve-digit confirmation of QED." Overstated in the opposite direction, and the correction matters. The measurement is 0.13 ppt (1); the test is limited by the imported value of alpha, whose uncertainty (0.229 x 10^-12) is twenty times the tenth-order QED uncertainty (0.011) (2), and whose two best determinations differ from the g/2 value by 2.1 and 3.9 sigma (1). The tenth-order coefficient itself needed an independent recomputation to resolve a 5 sigma discrepancy between two calculations (3). Both the triumphalist and the dismissive readings are wrong.

3. "Jets are simply assumed to be quarks." Not supported as stated. The jet energy scale is calibrated so that reconstructed jet energy equals the energy of the corresponding particle-level jet (27), anchored on single-particle test-beam response and in-data pT balance, to 0.3% at 300 GeV (27). The defensible claim is narrower: parton-level unfolding is a separate, model-dependent step, and the parton is never the object of a measurement. Use that form.

4. "Missing transverse energy is invented to rescue the theory." Not supported. It is a measured momentum imbalance with quantified resolution, improved up to 30% at high pile-up by working-point choice (28). What is model-licensed is the interpretation as an invisible particle, and separately the soft-term uncertainty is defined by the spread across three generators (28) - a real instance of theory input sizing an error bar, which is the claim worth making.

5. "The muon g-2 anomaly has been resolved." Not supported. It has been relocated. WP20 and WP25 are incompatible at about 3 sigma (8); the switch to lattice HVP happened because CMD-3 made data-driven combination "impossible... in a meaningful way" (6); KLOE and CMD-3 differ at 5 sigma (10); and the origin of that tension "remains unknown" (9).

6. "The muon g-2 anomaly is still a 5 sigma signal of new physics." Also not supported. Against WP25 the difference is 0.6 sigma (6)(7), and independent lattice groups agree with each other (11). Citing the 2020 comparison as current is a factual error.

7. "The CDF-II W mass is evidence for new physics." Not supported. PDG excludes it; inclusion drops compatibility from 91% to 0.5% (14); CMS disagrees with it by 73 MeV while agreeing with the SM (15); and in the global fit it forces m_t = 184.2 +/- 1.7 GeV against a measured 172.47 +/- 0.46 (17). The surviving critical point is governance - an unresolvable dispute over a decommissioned detector's dataset with no independent reanalysis (16).

8. "The B-meson anomalies have all evaporated." Not supported, and neither is the opposite. R(K) and R(K*) did dissolve, from 3.1 sigma to 0.2 sigma, with the cause published - electron identification and misidentified-hadron background modelling (59). The b -> s mu mu angular and branching-fraction anomalies persist, with the live dispute now being hadronic charm-loop contributions versus new physics, and a non-zero S7 that "cannot be explained by heavy new physics" (60).

9. "Supersymmetry has been ruled out by the LHC." Not supported. pMSSM-viable gluinos at about 1 TeV and stops at about 400 GeV survive, simplified-model limits assume two or three new particles with simple decays, and high-scale SUSY cannot be excluded by any feasible collider (41). The converse, "natural SUSY is alive and well," is also not supported: a 125 GeV Higgs in the MSSM needs a ~3 TeV stop scale or large mixing, in conflict with the naturalness motivation for light stops (41). The testable residue is higgsinos at mu <~ 200-400 GeV (41).

10. "Technicolor is a live alternative to the Higgs mechanism." Not supported. S parameter above the allowed value, ETC flavour bounds requiring 10^3-10^4 TeV, technipions too light against LEP, and no generic prediction of a 125 GeV scalar: "the original models are largely ruled out" (38). The surviving strong-dynamics programme has, on its own lattice evidence, no confirmed realistic model (39).

11. "MOND is a viable replacement for particle dark matter." Not supported, on its own advocates' account. Clusters are where "the apparent failure of MOND is most evident"; a quasi-static AQUAL/QUMOND limit "is not sufficient" for the Bullet Cluster; Cassini disfavours the simplest interpolating functions; and TeVeS "has thus been ruled out after GW170817" (45). The converse, "MOND has no successes," is also not supported: the baryonic Tully-Fisher relation has about 6% intrinsic scatter and the radial acceleration relation extends to weak lensing (45).

12. "Preon models and extra dimensions remain promising near-term targets." Not supported. Compositeness scales above 24-36 TeV and excited quarks excluded to 6.7 TeV (42); ADD M_D above 9.4 TeV with sub-37-micrometre radius limits (43); RS KK gravitons above 4.8 TeV (43). Each is beyond the reach of any approved machine, which makes them untestable rather than promising.

13. "Collider results are fabricated or are circular artefacts of the analysis chain." Not supported. Blind analysis with hidden offsets, independent detectors, test-beam and in-data calibration to 0.3%, published likelihoods (27)(36). And the record refutes it directly: the field published a 750 GeV bump and killed it (31), and published a 3.1 sigma R(K) anomaly and removed it with a published reason (59). The surviving Tier C claim is about correlated generator and PDF systematics across experiments with no external auditor (36)(37) - a different and much stronger argument.

14. "Quarks do not exist" / "confinement is an unfalsifiable excuse." Not supported. Bjorken scaling, the (1-y)^2 antineutrino distribution, the 5/18 rule, R = 11/3 against 11/9, gluon J = 1 from TASSO, nine lattice hadron masses from three inputs, and the top quark decaying before hadronisation (24)(25)(26). Recorded against the field's own usual framing: confinement has no rigorous proof, and Yang-Mills existence and the mass gap remains an open Clay Millennium Problem, with "no proof of this property... known" (62). That gap is real and should be stated whenever confinement is called established; it does not transfer any support to the fringe claim.

15. "Plasma cosmology and the electric universe are suppressed alternatives." Not supported. Plasma cosmology's CMB fit is 6 sigma from FIRAS, its helium-metal ratio off by a factor of four, its deuterium-lithium ratio off by about five orders of magnitude, its required absorption holes absent toward Cygnus A, Abell 2218 and 3CRR sources, and its number-count exponent -1.25 against an observed -1.8 (55). The electric universe is refuted by the measured solar neutrino spectrum, including CNO-cycle neutrinos at the predicted flux (56). These failed on evidence, not on reception.

16. "Santilli's dismissal of the neutrino and quark conjectures deserves a hearing on the merits." Not supported. It was examined (58) and it is contradicted by direct neutrino detection, measured oscillation parameters (23), species-resolved solar fluxes (56), and the 0.45 eV mass bound (49).

17. "The field does not acknowledge the model-dependence of its own results." Clearly false, and the claim would discredit this stream if made. Acknowledgement is institutional and in the collaborations' own documents: a multi-collaboration report stating that reinterpretation is impossible without information the experiments do not currently publish, and that ML-based analyses cannot be reproduced externally (37); a Harvard Data Science Review paper by a Fermilab and an Imperial physicist cataloguing the limits of reproduction and the primacy of unknown systematics (36); ATLAS stating in print that pT^miss is "a proxy" and that its soft-term uncertainty is assumed to be dominated by MC modelling (28); a published philosophical analysis, built from LHCb's own procedures, concluding that real and simulated data cannot easily be separated in the final data model (29); and a Fermilab theorist arguing in print that 5 sigma "does not hold up to historical scrutiny" (30). The defensible critical claim is not that the field is unaware. It is that this acknowledgement is distributed across dozens of documents with different audiences and has never been assembled into a single auditable accounting - and that the two quantities which would make it auditable, a cross-experiment correlation treatment for generator and PDF systematics and an acceptance measure over signature space, have no owner and no publication. C Falsified by: publication of either.

18. "The strongest current evidence for physics beyond the Standard Model is at colliders." Not supported. The strongest live tensions are in cosmology - dynamical dark energy at 3.1 sigma from BAO plus CMB and 2.8-4.2 sigma with supernovae (52), and the neutrino mass cosmology-oscillation tension at up to about 3 sigma (53) - plus the unexplained >5 sigma gallium deficit (48). The collider sector's two headline anomalies have respectively dissolved (59) and been reduced to a measurement dispute (14)(16). This ordering is worth carrying into the governance stream.
